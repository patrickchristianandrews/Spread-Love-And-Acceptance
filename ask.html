Chat with Professor Puddles

Type a question in your own words, like “what is Unbilled Debt?” or “how do we stop arguing about chores?”, and Professor Puddles, a small drop with a big brain, will answer with passages from this site, each with a link to read the whole page.

  • Only from these pages. The Professor doesn’t make things up. If the site doesn’t cover something, he’ll say so and point you to what’s close.
  • What you type stays on this device. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the conversation is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Not a counselor. It’s a friendly way to find your way around the site, not advice for your situation.

Prefer to browse? The Contents and its site directory list every page, and Start here is a gentle first step.
