Ask the buddies

Type a question in your own words, like “what is Unbilled Debt?” or “how do we stop arguing about chores?”, and a buddy will answer with passages from this site, each with a link to read the whole page.

  • Only from these pages. The buddies don’t make things up. If the site doesn’t cover something, they’ll say so and point you to what’s close.
  • What you type stays on this device. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the conversation is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Not a counsellor. It’s a friendly way to find your way around the site, not advice for your situation. If you’re afraid of someone, or struggling right now, please see where to get help.

Prefer to browse? The Contents lists every page, and Start here is a gentle first step.
