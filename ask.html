How Professor Puddles answers

He doesn’t make things up. Professor Puddles isn’t a generative AI that writes new text. He is a small scripted helper that runs right here in your browser.

  • Every answer is drawn from this site’s own pages, cards and library, written and checked by people, with a link to read more. When an answer comes from his background notes rather than a page, he says so.
  • Research he mentions is named honestly, and the site is adding a page of checked sources.
  • He can still pick the wrong topic. If he misses, tell him “that’s not it” or say it another way.
  • He isn’t a therapist and can’t diagnose. For anything about safety, he points you to real help.

What he can do

Ten things to try. You don’t need the right words: type it the way you’d say it to a friend.

  • Your situation, your words

    Couples, friends, family, co-parents, housemates, work. Describe what’s happening and get a few kind next steps.

  • Finds the right tool or page

    Every tool, workpaper, chapter and game: what it’s for, how to use it and what your results mean.

  • Words you could use

    Short sentences you could say out loud or send, for raising something hard, saying no or saying sorry.

  • Just chat

    Small talk when you want company: how he’s doing, a riddle, a fun fact. Say “just chat” to start.

  • Talk deep

    Big questions, like what love is or whether honesty is always kind, through philosophy, psychology and an autistic lens.

  • Makes infographics

    A small poster about a topic, drawn on your device, that you can save as an image, share or print.

  • Safety first

    If something sounds like fear or control, not a communication problem, he says so gently and points to real help.

  • Explains ideas plainly

    The mental load, fair vs equal, “flooded”, the double empathy problem: what they mean, with an everyday example.

  • Jokes on request

    Very bad pond puns, only when you ask. Never in the middle of something hard.

  • Un poco de español

    Ask in Spanish and he’ll answer the basics in Spanish, with the site’s Spanish page.

Good to know

Private by design

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere. This tab remembers the conversation until you close it, and then it’s gone. Pictures you save stay on your device too.

What he can’t do

He can’t diagnose, and he isn’t therapy or professional advice. He can be wrong or pick the wrong topic: tell him “that’s not it”, or say it another way. You know your situation best.

If you don’t feel safe

If someone frightens you, controls you or hurts you, that isn’t a communication problem. Not safe at home? has free, private help. The Leave quickly button, or Esc twice, takes you off this site fast.

Starting over

Tap Start over at the top of the chat for a fresh conversation. Closing the tab clears it too. In deep talk, say “small talk” to come back to lighter chat.

Prefer to browse? The Contents and its site directory list every page, and Start here is a gentle first step.
