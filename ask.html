What the Professor can help with

Type a question in your own words, like “how do I use the Lemonade Stand?”, “what does my battery score mean?” or “my coworker keeps interrupting me”, and Professor Puddles, a small drop with a big brain, will answer from this program, with links to the right pages.

  • Knows the whole program. Every tool, workpaper, chapter and game: what it’s for, how to use it step by step, and what your results mean. Type your Battery Meter answers or CALC-01 numbers and he’ll work out the score with you.
  • Kind next steps for what’s going on. Describe a situation, with yourself or someone else, and he’ll suggest a few steps, words you could use and a short path on the site. You know your situation best.
  • Background notes, clearly labelled. When the site doesn’t cover something, the Professor has notes on related topics, like negotiation, habits or co-parenting logistics. Answers from them always say so, and point you to a page to put it into practice.
  • What you type stays on this device. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere, and the conversation is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Not a counselor. It’s general guidance and a friendly way around the site, not therapy or professional advice.
