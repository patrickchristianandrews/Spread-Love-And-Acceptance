# Sugarfoot reference page

`sugarfoot-ref.png` is the owner's storybook picture of Sugarfoot. It is the style target for her.

- `python3 sfline.py` builds the line art and a colored version from the picture. The fur borders come from the picture itself; the eyes, nose, mouth, ears, claws, toes and chest stripe are drawn by hand on top so they stay crisp. `sf-mask.png` is her cut-out silhouette.
- `python3 sfpage.py` puts her on an 850 x 1100 coloring page (`sf-page-line.svg`) and a colored sample (`sf-page-col.svg`).

# Tidbit reference page

`tidbit-ref.png` is the owner's storybook picture of Tidbit standing in the snow. `tb-mask.png` is his cut-out.

- `python3 tbline.py` builds his line art and colored version: silhouette and tan markings from the picture; eyes, nose, mouth, ears, collar, toes and claws drawn by hand.
- `python3 tbpage.py` puts him on the snow-day coloring page (`tb-page-line.svg`) and the colored sample (`tb-page-col.svg`).
