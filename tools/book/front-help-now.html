If you might act on thoughts of ending your life, or someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number: 999 in the UK, 112 or 999 in Ireland, 911 in the US and Canada, 000 in Australia, 111 in New Zealand.

If you are struggling, or worried about someone else, you can talk to a trained person now. These lines are free, private and open day and night. You don’t have to be sure it is a crisis to call or text.

Where you areCall or text
United StatesCall or text 988 (the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at 988lifeline.org. You can also text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
United KingdomCall Samaritans free on 116 123. Or text SHOUT to 85258 (Shout, a free text line).
IrelandCall Samaritans free on 116 123.
CanadaCall or text 9-8-8 (the Suicide Crisis Helpline), in English or French.
AustraliaCall Lifeline on 13 11 14, or text 0477 13 11 14.
New ZealandCall or text 1737 (Need to talk?).
Anywhere elsefindahelpline.com lists free helplines in many countries, by phone, text and chat.

If you’re worried about someone

Ask them plainly: “Are you thinking about ending your life?” Studies have found that asking does not put the idea in someone’s head, and talking about it may even ease it. Listen, stay with them if you can, and call or text one of the lines above together. {{ref:understanding-12-helping#crisis|Part Three, Chapter 12}} says more about helping someone in a crisis, and {{ref:understanding-16-worry#more-help|Chapter 16}} about worry, low mood and when to get help.

This book is general education, not a crisis service. The website’s tools keep what you type on your own device, so nobody there can see it or reply to it. The numbers were checked in October 2026. Numbers can change; findahelpline.com keeps an up-to-date list.
