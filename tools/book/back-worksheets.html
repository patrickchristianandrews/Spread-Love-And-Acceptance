Say it so it lands

Fillable version: spreadloveandacceptance.com/workpapers/fill/wp-09.html

A self-check each of you uses on your own side of a conversation. Use it on one thing that actually stung.

Your nameDate

Before you speak or send: fact, feeling, ask

Raw reaction (optional, and just for you)
What’s the fact underneath this? (One sentence, no adjectives.) For example: “The trash went out late on Tuesday.”
What’s the feeling underneath this? (Frustrated, tired, unseen, rushed…) A feeling about you, not a verdict about them.
What’s the actual ask? (What do you want to happen next, specifically?) Something the other person can say yes to.

Before you react: four quick checks

CheckCircle one
Is this about a specific, nameable task or event?Yes · No
Would I read this the same way if I weren’t already carrying a lot?Yes · No · Not sure
Is there a neutral interpretation that also fits what was said?Yes · No
Am I responding to their words, or to a pattern from a past conversation?Their words · A past pattern

A “No” or “Not sure” on the second check, a “Yes” on the third or “A past pattern” on the fourth are good reasons to pause first. A kind “not right now” and a time to come back keep it honest.
