Guided journeys

Short paths of three to six small steps, for what’s going on in your life. Each step says what you’ll do, about how long it takes, and what you’ll leave with: a script, a plan, an agreement or a card. The journeys you can do on your own come first. Everything is free, and nothing you type leaves your device.

What’s going on?

Tap the closest. You’ll get one journey that fits, and one thing you can do in the next two minutes.

On your own

Start here even if someone else is involved. People usually explore on their own first.

With someone

For two people, or a family. Either of you can start alone and invite the other in later. Steps marked “Workpaper” are optional fill-in worksheets, kept separate from the tools.

How the journeys work

  • Labels: “Tool” opens a tool. “Read” opens a short page. “Workpaper” opens a fill-in worksheet, and is always optional.
  • Ticks stay on this device. When you tick a step, only the tick and the date you started are kept here. Nothing you write in a tool is copied into the journey. You can clear it on On this device.
  • Come back in a week. If you ask, the next time you visit after that date we’ll ask one short question, like “Did the agreement hold?”, on the home page and here. You can also add a reminder to your own calendar. It lives in your calendar; nothing is sent to us.
  • Doing it with someone? This is for understanding each other, not for scoring points. Either of you can stop at any time. Nothing here is a verdict about a person. Share only what you’re comfortable sharing. More on working on it together.

Not sure any of these fit? Three quick questions point you to a first step, or ask Professor Puddles in your own words. All the tools are on one page.
