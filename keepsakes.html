Your garden

Petals, and the garden they grow

Every calm game you play earns petals, a few more when you play well. Petals never run out or get spent. They add up, and at each milestone your garden grows: something new and peaceful appears in the living garden behind every page on the site.

Tile colours

For Quiet Words, Word Bloom and Quiet Crossword. Tap one to use it.

Your garden’s wonders

Each one appears in the garden behind every page, and in the Night Garden, as soon as you grow it. You might spot the two dogs playing by them. You can switch any of them off.

Ways to earn petals

  • Word Bloom: petals for every level, more for a perfect bloom with no hints, and a bonus jar that pays out every ten bonus words.
  • Quiet Crossword: petals for each puzzle, more when you finish without revealing letters.
  • Quiet Words: petals for every puzzle, more when you find every word without a hint.
  • The Night Garden: petals for every four calm breaths, each constellation, shooting stars, and every row that blooms on the lily pond (double and triple blooms earn more).
  • Today’s bouquet: one calm breath, one word game and one garden game in a day earns 10 bonus petals. See it on Pause & Play.
  • A breathing break: finishing one anywhere on the site earns petals too.
  • Your daily blossom: the first game each day brings a few extra petals, and a few more for each day in your streak, up to a week.
  • A gentle streak: miss one day in a week and it counts as a rest day. Your streak carries on.

Kept on this device: your petals and your garden live in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is nothing to buy.
