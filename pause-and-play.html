Breathe with the circle

What is Pause & Play? It’s the calm corner of the site: a handful of gentle games for when your mind needs a rest. Breathe in the Night Garden, take the Frequency Journey, spell words in Word Bloom, solve a crossword, or find words in a quiet word search. There are no timers and no way to lose.

Every level you finish counts, and now and then something new appears in the living garden behind every page and joins the animals playing there. My garden keeps a list of everything that has arrived. A few minutes here is a lovely way to settle before a hard conversation, or to wind down at night. Nothing you do is sent anywhere.
