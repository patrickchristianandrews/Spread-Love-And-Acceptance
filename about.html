Hi, I'm Christian

Simple versionRead the full story

Christian spent fifteen years as a government auditor and a lifetime trying to understand how people work. The Objective Ledger grew out of both.

  1. I spent fifteen years untangling complicated systems.

    My job was looking closely at how government programs really worked, as an auditor and audit manager. My longer study has been people. I studied psychology, philosophy, neurobiology and more to understand how mind, body and surroundings fit together.

    Dig deeper: where it started

  2. My brain speaks a different language.

    I am autistic, and my wiring includes OCD, ADHD and anxiety. It gives me fast pattern-spotting and strong logic. (Being neurotypical is awesome too!)

    Dig deeper: a different language

  3. Hiding it took a lot out of me.

    For years I masked who I was to keep others comfortable. It wore me out, and my directness was often misread as harshness, which left me feeling unseen.

    Dig deeper: the cost of masking

  4. Dropping the mask changed everything.

    I learned what my body and senses need, and I stay steady with self-care routines built around them.

    Dig deeper: dropping the mask

  5. Now I want to help you do the same.

    The Objective Ledger is built from that experience, so you can map your own reality and build a life where your true wiring can thrive.

    Dig deeper: the mission

  6. Nine fields each shaped a tool.

    Each field I studied became part of the program. My auditing work, for example, became “One owner per job” (WP-03), which gives every regular task one named person.

    Dig deeper: the nine inputs

Those nine fields come together as the Five Pillars: see the whole load, fix the setup not the person, read your state first, tune how you send and receive, and notice the quiet incentives. Each one starts inside you and then shows up between you and other people.
