Christian spent fifteen years as a government auditor and a lifetime trying to understand how people work. The Objective Ledger grew out of both.

    I was a government auditor and audit manager. My longer audit has been of people: I studied psychology, philosophy, neurobiology and more to understand how mind, body and surroundings fit together.

  2. My brain speaks a different language.
