About

The Auditor

Christian — founder, Spread Love and Acceptance

15 Years in Audit · A Lifetime in the Other One

IThe Mandate

For fifteen years, I built a career as a government auditor and audit manager specializing in tax enforcement, internal controls, and compliance. My professional life was defined by a singular mandate: finding the objective truth inside highly complex, opaque systems.

But my most intense, lifelong audit hasn't been financial; it has been a relentless, forensic quest to understand the architecture of the human experience. Driven by a burning desire to map how our internal and external worlds intersect, I have obsessively studied psychology, philosophy, behavioral science, neurobiology, economics, business, finance, alternative holistic therapies, and aromatherapy. I needed to understand the exact mechanics of the mind, the body, and the environments we exist in.

Psychology Philosophy Behavioral Science Neurobiology Economics Business Finance Holistic Therapies Aromatherapy

IIA Different Language

My brain inherently speaks a different language. I am autistic, and my specific neurological wiring includes OCD, ADHD, and anxiety. I possess a unique, multi-threaded thinking process drenched in creativity that deviates dramatically from a neurotypical mind (and for the record, being neurotypical is awesome too!). This divergence gave me extraordinary cognitive gifts: high-speed pattern recognition, uncompromising logic, and the ability to operate coherently with adaptive technology. When leveraged correctly, I can channel this processing power for good, driving tasks and systemic problem-solving to near-perfect execution.

IIIThe Invisible Cost

But that horsepower came with a crushing, invisible cost. Since I was a kid, I absorbed the unspoken societal message that it wasn't okay to just be me. I spent my life aggressively masking my neurodivergence to make everyone else comfortable, sacrificing my own identity and peace in front of the world. Running a high-velocity cognitive engine while simultaneously pretending to run a standard operating system led to profound, debilitating exhaustion.

IVRelational Static

The heaviest toll of this exhaustion was exacted on my personal life. The process of forming and keeping relationships became a source of severe relational static. Because of the way I am inherently wired, my high-resolution directness and rapid pattern synthesis were constantly misinterpreted. My genuine intentions to provide clarity, operational truth, and structural support were misunderstood as intensity, aggression, or a lack of empathy.

This constant mistranslation led to a very lonely and tortured existence — a feeling of being entirely unseen despite standing right in front of people I cared for deeply.

VDropping the Mask

Yet, as I look back, none of that pain went in vain. My uncompromising logic demanded that I learn from every bad outcome and leverage it for good. Through pivotal life events, relentless study, and the crucible of my career, I finally found absolute clarity. I realized that to survive, I had to drop the mask. I had to stop living for everyone else and start honoring my own biological reality. I worked through the burnout to learn precisely what I need to do to manage the taxing sides of my gifts.

Today, my equilibrium is sustained by deeply individualized self-care routines based on my specific sensory and bodily needs, utilizing holistic practices to keep my nervous system regulated and grounded.

VIThe Mission

Through Spread Love and Acceptance and the foundational framework of The Objective Ledger™ (TOL‑OS), I now use my lived experience to help others map their own realities. I stopped trying to force my brain to run an incompatible operating system and instead built an environment where my true wiring could thrive — and my mission is to help you do the exact same thing.

God, thank you for the allowance of the presence of my angels who work so hard; I feel their love every day with every step. I feel blessed, sanctified.

If any of this sounds like your own wiring, or like the static in a relationship you care about, the framework built out of it is here: the nervous system self-check and the shared register are free and take a few minutes. The companion album carries what the numbers were never going to reach.
