How to play: drag your finger from petal to petal to spell a word, then let go. Slide back to undo a letter. You can also tap petals one at a time and tap the middle, or type on a keyboard. 🔀 mixes up the letters, and 💡 shows one letter. Finish a level without hints for a perfect bloom.
