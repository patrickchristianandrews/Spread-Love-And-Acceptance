Word Bloom: a relaxing word game

✨ 🌼 Bloom ✏️ Crossword 📰 Newspaper 🔍 Words

Swipe across the petals to spell words and fill the little crossword. Any everyday word that fits a slot fills it, even if it isn’t the one we had in mind, and other real words go in your bonus jar. No timer, no way to lose, and now and then something new in the garden behind the page. Thousands of wheels, so you’ll rarely see the same one twice.

How to play: drag your finger from petal to petal to spell a word, then let go. Slide back to undo a letter. You can also tap petals one at a time and tap the middle, or type on a keyboard. 🔀 mixes up the letters, and 💡 shows one letter, as often as you like. If spelling is tiring, turn on “First letters” to see where each word starts, or tap “Hear a word” to hear one of the words out loud.
