What travels, and what doesn’t

  • Only what you choose to send. A link holds your part as it is when you send it; after a change, send a new one.
  • Private things are never offered by default: today’s weather and anything you wrote for one person only go if you tick them yourself, and they’re marked “private”.
  • What someone sends you stays in that tab, unless you tick “Keep on this device”. “Clear what you received” removes it.
  • Very long links don’t fit in a QR code or a text message. Then you can copy it as a code, or send it by email.
