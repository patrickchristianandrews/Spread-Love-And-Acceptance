Someone you know sent you this link. Here is what this site is, in three lines:

  • A free, private place to understand yourself and the people in your life: short guides, a book, small tools and a friendly helper you can ask anything.
  • It looks at the setup and how each person is wired (who does what, how tired each person is, how words land), never at who is to blame.
  • It works for partners, co-parents, friends, family, housemates, coworkers and people on their own. No account, nothing to sign up for.
  • You don’t have to do anything.Reading this page is enough. “Not now” and “no thanks” are both fine answers.
  • Nobody sees your answers.Anything you type stays on this device, in this browser. Not the person who sent this, not us. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.
  • You are not the problem.Sending this usually means “I’d like things to feel lighter for both of us”. The site is written for both of you.
  • Nobody is the problem.Sending this usually means “I’d like handoffs and messages to go more smoothly, for the kids’ sake”. The co-parenting guide is written for both homes, and it never asks who was right.

Sending this to someone?

Pick a message, change the words so they sound like you, and send it from your own phone. Nothing is sent from here. The link in each one opens this page.

  • Send it at a calm moment, not in the middle of an argument.
  • Ask for one small thing, not the whole site.
  • Let “not now” be a full answer. You can still try things on your own.

If they say no or not yet, that’s an answer, not a door closing. Everything on this site also works for one person: Start in 10 minutes on your own.
