Someone sent you this

Someone you know sent you this link. Here is what it is, in three lines:

  • A free site of short reads and small tools for sharing everyday life more kindly.
  • It looks at the setup (who does what, how tired each person is, how words land), never at who is to blame.
  • It works for partners, friends, family, housemates, coworkers and people on their own.
  • You don’t have to do anything.Reading this page is enough. “Not now” and “no thanks” are both fine answers.
  • Nobody sees your answers.Anything you type stays on your phone, in this browser. Not the person who sent this, not us. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.
  • You are not the problem.Sending this usually means “I’d like things to feel lighter for both of us”. The site is written for both of you.

If you’d like to try one thing

  • About 2 minutes · just for you
    Today’s WeatherA few taps on how you are doing today: sleep, stress, what would help. It reads you, never anyone else, and you don’t have to show it to anyone.
  • Just read · about 3 minutes
    What they can and can’t seeThe longer version of this page, with kind ways to say yes, not yet or no.

That’s all. You can close this page now, or come back any time. Nothing here keeps track.

If you feel pressured, or you’re afraid of the person who sent this, you don’t owe them a yes, and these tools aren’t meant for that. Not safe at home? has free, private helplines and a quick way to leave this site.

Sending this to someone?

Pick a message, change the words so they sound like you, and send it from your own phone. Nothing is sent from here. The link in each one opens this page.

  • Send it at a calm moment, not in the middle of an argument.
  • Ask for one small thing, not the whole site.
  • Let “not now” be a full answer. You can still try things on your own.

If they say no or not yet, that’s an answer, not a door closing. Everything on this site also works for one person: Start in 10 minutes on your own.
