Frequency Calibration & Coherence AuditTOL-WP04-SPEC-V1.0

Map the operating frequencies where the partnership runs. Identify where they're out of sync. Suggest coherence mechanisms to align them.

What this measures: A partnership is a system made of overlapping oscillators—think of it like two radios running at slightly different frequencies. When they're in sync, there's clarity. When they drift out of phase, they create interference and a "squeal" (the beat frequency). Finance, autonomics, decisions, communication, and philosophy all oscillate at their own rates. Every friction point is usually a frequency mismatch, not a values conflict.

The good news: Mismatched frequencies aren't a failure—they're information. If you both know the frequencies, you can build coherence mechanisms (shared rhythms, locked cycles, synchronized check-ins) to synchronize the oscillators.

What this is not: This is not a diagnostic tool, a compatibility test, or a predictor of relationship viability. It's a systems audit: a description of how the machine is currently running, so you can both make deliberate choices about which frequencies need to align.

Coherence Action Plan

The oscillators creating the largest beat frequencies (visual chart above) are your primary friction points. Work through them in this order: autonomic first (most urgent), then communicational, then decisional, financial, and finally philosophical.

Immediate (This Week)

  • Starting with any autonomic mismatches (highest urgency), read the coherence recommendation for that domain.
  • If autonomic delta is above 1.5x, implement WP-13 (Phase-Locked Loop) 90-second syncs immediately. This is your beat-frequency dampener.

This Month

  • For each medium-to-large delta (yellow/red in the chart), follow the specific coherence recommendation above. Each one names the tool that synchronizes that oscillator.
  • Communicational first: Lock WP-13 (Phase-Locked Loop Protocol) into your calendar. This synchronizes check-in frequency.
  • Then decisional: Complete WP-03 (Domestic RACI Treaty) to define which decisions lock immediately vs. revisit cycles.
  • Then financial: Run CALC-01 (Solvency Read) every Friday to track whether your cash-flow rhythm is sustainable.

Every Quarter

  • Re-run this audit. Circumstances change (new job, illness, seasons, life stage), and your frequencies may shift. Track whether your deltas are converging or diverging.
  • If philosophical frequency was "never," use WP-13's quarterly alignment blocks to make it "once per quarter" explicitly. Implicit alignment drifts. Scheduled alignment holds.

Important: This is a heuristic self-report tool, not a validated diagnostic instrument. It describes observed operating frequencies, not underlying pathology or incompatibility. Large frequency deltas are normal in human partnerships; they're not a sign of failure. What matters is whether you both know about them and are willing to build coherence mechanisms. This tool is only useful if both partners fill it in independently, then sit down and compare notes with genuine curiosity.
