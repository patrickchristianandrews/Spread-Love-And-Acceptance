What’s new

The site keeps growing. Here’s everything newly added, and everything that’s been expanded, with the newest first. Tap any card to go straight there.

🌱 Newly added

Brand-new pages and things to try.

  • 🏛The Five Pillars NEW

    The five ideas under everything here, how each one works inside you and between you and others, and a map of which pillars every chapter, workpaper and tool puts to work.

  • 🌀Drift: the calm visualizer NEW

    Pick how you feel, put on headphones, and float through slow, deep 3D colors while binaural tones and a breathing guide ease you toward calm.

  • 📚The Professor’s Library NEW

    233 short, plain-language entries on psychology, behavioral science and conflict resolution, each tied to the program. Professor Puddles can chat about all of them.

  • 📰Something to read NEW

    Hand-picked articles from Psychology Today, Greater Good and the Gottman Institute, for understanding yourself or getting on with others.

  • 🎵The Frequency Journey NEW

    A calm puzzle journey with two best friends through seven tone-themed worlds. Expect riddles, life lessons, memory games, melodies and puzzles, with a different challenge every level, getting gently harder as you climb.

  • 🎧Four soundscapes NEW

    The Breath Beneath, One Breath to Anchor You, Nothing Needs to Change and The Road We Made, in the order they’re best used, each with what it’s good for.

  • 💬Chat with Professor Puddles NEW

    Ask a question in your own words and get an answer made only from this site’s pages, with a link to read more. What you type stays on your device.

  • ✨Levels that grow the background NEW

    Every few levels in the calm games, something new appears in the garden behind every page (a swing, koi, a bridge, a gazebo and more), and it joins in with the animals playing there.

  • 📰The Daily Ledger Crossword NEW

    A newspaper-style crossword, from a quick Mini to a Big Sunday, with hundreds of puzzles.

  • 🌼Word Bloom and Quiet Crossword NEW

    Two calm word games with difficulty levels, a random puzzle any time, and a tip from the program after every level.

  • 📑The Workpaper Suite NEW

    Every workpaper as a fillable, printable PDF, gathered for the road you’re on, with a week-by-week plan and a full report written for each kind of relationship.

🌊 Expanded and deeper

Parts of the site that now go further than before.

  • 💧Mini dives, from the shore to the deep DEEPER

    Tap a word with the little water drop, anywhere in the course. There are now 172 of them, and each one wades in step by step: the shore, toes in the water, the shallows, waist deep, then the full page.

  • 📖Chapters that keep growing GROWING

    Every chapter of the book now grows in place: as each part of the program is developed, new material is added right inside that chapter.

  • 🥰Little friends as you read MORE

    Cheerful characters, often in silly disguises, sit between the sections of reading pages. They cheer you on, share a “did you know?” that fits the page, and some will chat.

  • 🌄A scene for every part of the site MORE

    A beach, a mountain lake, a flower meadow, a river and a pine forest, each lit by your own clock, from dawn to night. The Night Garden always stays night.

  • 🌙A livelier garden MORE

    The garden behind every page is full of new adventures: capes and flying saucers, a little plane, kites, catch, water balloons, swims, naps in the hammock and dances in the gazebo.
