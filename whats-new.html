🕓 Newest: Complacency

Added on October 4, 2026. What happens in the long stretch where nothing seems wrong, and how to keep things alive.

  • 🕓Complacency: when “it’s fine” stops being checked NEW

    October 4, 2026

    Complacency isn’t laziness. It’s what happens when something has worked for a long time and we stop looking. The quiet signs, how it shows up in you, partners, family, friends, housemates and coworkers, how it differs from contentment and acceptance, and small, kind re-checks. Also woven into the relationships guide, Know your own wiring, Turning Toward, Check-ins, the Five Pillars, the book’s chapters and the Library. It also covers how to raise it with someone, with context from both sides, and touchstones: the shared words, jokes and stories that say “we’re us.”

  • 👾The Frequency Journey is now an arcade NEW

    October 4, 2026

    Pick Tidbit or Sugarfoot, then a game: Treat Chase, a maze where you gather every treat while friendly-ish clouds of Static drift about, or Cross the Way, where you hop over a busy road and ride the logs and lily pads to five doghouses. Steer with the arrow keys, a swipe, a tap or big on-screen arrows. No game over: a bump just sends your pup back to the start. Frequency Buddies lives on the same page.

  • 🏈The Re-check Drive NEW

    October 4, 2026

    A calm football game made from the complacency playbook. Do a small, kind thing for real (notice, ask, thank, share your side, ask for theirs) and the ball moves toward a field goal. No clock, no way to lose, and nothing you type leaves your device.
