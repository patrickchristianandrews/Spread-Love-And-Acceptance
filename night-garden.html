Why a garden?

The Night Garden brings together a few things that research suggests can quiet a busy mind. It isn’t treatment, and it doesn’t replace talking to someone. It’s a gentle place to land.

  • Slow breathing. Breathing at around six breaths a minute (in for 4, out for 6) is one of the most reliable ways to settle the body. The light in the garden keeps that pace for you.
  • Gentle play. Relaxed, casual games have been linked with better mood and lower stress. There’s no score to chase here and nothing to lose.
  • Busy hands, quiet mind. Fitting shapes together takes up the same mental room that worry loops use, which is why the lily pond can help a racing mind slow down.
  • Soft, natural movement. Watching gently moving light, water and leaves lets tired attention rest.

The garden grows as you visit: flowers you bloom stay, the moon follows the real one, and the seasons change with the calendar. Missing a day costs nothing. Everything is kept only in this browser, on this device.
