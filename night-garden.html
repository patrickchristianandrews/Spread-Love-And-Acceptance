    Why a garden?

    The Night Garden is a gentle place to land at the end of a day: a few quiet things to do, with nothing to win and nothing to lose. It isn’t a treatment, and it doesn’t replace talking to someone.

    • Slow breathing. The garden brings a different calm pattern on most visits: box breathing (in, hold, out, hold, four counts each), a triangle, a slow wave or a long breath out. Each one breathes out for at least as long as it breathes in, at a few unhurried breaths a minute. The light in the garden keeps the count for you, so all you have to do is follow it.
    • Gentle play. Fireflies to guide and lilies to fit together, at your own pace. There’s no score to chase here and nothing to lose.
    • Busy hands. Fitting shapes together gives your attention one small, absorbing thing to hold, if your thoughts feel busy.
    • Soft, natural movement. Slowly moving light, water and leaves, to watch for as long as you like.

    The garden grows as you visit: flowers you bloom stay, the moon follows the real one, the seasons change with the calendar, and every night has its own sky. The pictures, ponds and breathing patterns you’ve seen are remembered, so they don’t come round again too soon. Missing a day costs nothing. Everything is kept only in this browser, on this device.
