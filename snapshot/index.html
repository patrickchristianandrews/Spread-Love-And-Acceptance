Seven quick questions about how a shared load is set up, and how it bends when things get heavy.

The snapshot looks through all Five Pillars, starting with Pillar I, See the whole load and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person. In you: you take an honest look at what you carry. Between you and others: you see the pattern as a setup you can change together, not a fault in anyone.

It works for any shared load: a partner, family at home, roommates, people sharing care for someone, or a team at work. It's a kind look at how things are, from your point of view, today. It isn't a test, and it doesn't diagnose anything or anyone.

Take the 2-minute snapshot

What you'll get

  • Which of four areas you're in right now: Steady, Ready to readjust, Could bend more or Carrying a lot right now
  • How it was worked out: two simple scores, one for the setup and one for how it bends, shown openly
  • One small step to try this week, and which pillar it belongs to
  • A copy you can print or paste, so each person can compare their own snapshot

Nothing you pick is saved or sent.
