The 2-minute snapshot

Seven questions about how a shared load is set up, and how it bends when things get heavy.

The snapshot looks through all Five Pillars. Questions 1 to 3 are about the setup: Pillar I, See the whole load and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person. Questions 4 to 6 are about how it bends: Pillar III, Read your state first, Pillar IV, Tune how you send and receive and Pillar V, Notice the quiet incentives. In you: an honest look at what you carry. Between you and others: a pattern you can change together, not a fault in anyone.

This is not a test and not a diagnosis. It's your view, today. It works best when each person takes it on their own and then you compare. Nothing you pick is saved or sent.
