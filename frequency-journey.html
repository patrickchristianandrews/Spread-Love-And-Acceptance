Meet the pals

Two best friends, each wonderful in her own way. They get there as themselves, and they get there side by side.

The one with the collar

Tidbit

Quick, curious and always a step ahead, with round tan eyebrows that go up when she’s thinking. She notices the small things, keeps an eye on her pal, and is first in line for a high five.

The one with the floppy ears

Sugarfoot

Soft ears, big white toes and an even bigger heart. She’s steady, brave in the dark and the best hugger there is, and she’s happiest right beside Tidbit.

Seven worlds, one song

Each world is tuned to its own tone, with its own kind of challenge and its own little lesson. The climb gets gently harder as you go.

    It’s easier to be brave next to someone you trust.

    Kindness to yourself makes room for joy.

    What one of us does changes the way for the other.

    Say it clearly and kindly, and it can be heard.

    Look again: what you can see is not always what you assumed.
  7. 963

    The Infinite Sky

    The top of the journey: an open sky to rest in, place stars and simply be, together.

    The Perfect Frequency was the two of you, in tune.

How the journey works

A new challenge every level

Riddles, little life lessons, sorting, memory, melodies, logic and path puzzles, each one different from the last.

No timers, no losing

A wrong answer just gets a kind explanation and another try. Hints are always there, and so are undo and restart.

Something to take with you

Every level ends with a small lesson from the program, and a link to read more if you’d like to.

Ready when you are

Put your headphones on if you have them. Each world plays its tone softly, and the pals are already waiting at the edge of the forest.
