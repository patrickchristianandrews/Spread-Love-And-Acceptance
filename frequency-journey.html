What is this? A calm puzzle journey through seven tone-themed worlds. Guide your two pals, together, all the way up to the Perfect Frequency. No timer, and no way to lose: you can always undo or start a level again.

How to play
  • Bring both pals to the glowing tone. It has room for two, and they arrive together.
  • Move by swiping on the board, with the arrow pad, or with the arrow keys (or W A S D). Tapping near a pal also takes one step that way.
  • Switch pal (or tap a pal, or press E) chooses who moves. Together (or T) moves both pals the same way at once.
  • Undo (Z) and Restart (R) are always there, and A gentle hint (H) shows one good next step.
  • Each world adds one new idea: lanterns and thorny shadows, slippery ice and melting blocks, light to gather, bridges raised by plates, crystals that sing a melody, and stars that aren’t quite what they seem. The last world is simply a place to rest and play.

The tones are used here as calm themes for each world, not as a treatment. Your progress stays in this browser on this device.
