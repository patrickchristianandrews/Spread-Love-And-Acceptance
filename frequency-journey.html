Meet the pals

Two best friends, each wonderful in her own way. They’re on the same team in every game.

The one with the mask

Tidbit

A black mask with little tan eyebrow dots, and a grin you can see from across the garden. She’s quick, curious and always a step ahead, keeps an eye on her pal, and is first in line for a high five. Underneath all that zoom is a big heart of gold.

The one with the white feet

Sugarfoot

Soft floppy ears, sweet white feet (that’s where her name comes from) and a slow, happy wag. She’s steady, brave in the dark and the best hugger there is, with a big heart of gold, and she’s happiest right beside Tidbit.

How the arcade works

Easy to control

Arrow keys, WASD, a swipe, a tap on the field or big on-screen arrows. A turn pressed a little early is remembered, and in Cross the Way one hop can wait for the last.

No game over

A bump or a splash sends your pup back to the start with a soft “oof”. Treats you’ve gathered stay gathered, and your score never goes down.

Yours alone

Your pup, your settings and your best scores stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Pause any time, and Relaxed pace slows everything down.

Ready when you are

Put your headphones on if you have them. Each world plays its tone softly, and the pals are already waiting at the edge of the forest.
