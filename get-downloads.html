One quick step before your download

The book PDFs, the logic guide and the workpaper PDFs ask for your email address once on this device. Everything stays free, and reading or filling things in on the site never needs an email.

Your email

Your address goes to Buttondown, our email service, for the optional email updates: at most one email a week, with one small idea and anything new on the site. Buttondown may send a confirmation email first. You can unsubscribe from any email in one tap.

This device remembers only that you’ve given an email here (not the address itself), so you won’t be asked again on it. Privacy policy.

You’re all set

Go back to the page you were on and tap the download button again. Your download will start right away. You can close this tab.
