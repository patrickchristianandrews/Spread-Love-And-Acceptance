Quiet Words: a calm word search with no timer

✨ 🌼 Bloom ✏️ Crossword 📰 Newspaper 🔍 Words

A gentle word search. There’s no timer, no score and no way to lose. Each word you find glows and leaves a kind thought behind. Music is off unless you turn it on. A new theme each day (the same for everyone), hundreds more to choose from, and the same grid every time you open a puzzle. If you step away, your place and the words you found are kept on this device. Now and then, something new arrives in the garden behind the page.

Theme

    Why it helps: a gentle puzzle gives your mind one simple, absorbing thing to do, which leaves less room for worry. Take it slowly. Notice each word, and the thought it leaves behind.
