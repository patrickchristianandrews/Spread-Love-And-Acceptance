Workpapers

The full path package and your report

One fillable PDF with every workpaper for your situation, and a detailed report written from your own answers. Use it on your own, or with 2 to 8 people: a partner, family, co-parent, friends, roommates, a team, or the people sharing care.

How it works

  1. Choose your road. Individual, Partners, Family, Co-parents, Friends, Roommates, Coworkers or Caregivers. Each road has the workpapers that fit it, in a sensible order, worded for that kind of relationship.
  2. Download one PDF. A cover and how-to, who’s on the road, every workpaper, the numbers for “Is the setup working for both of you?”, optional self-notes (your Wiring Card and a weather and battery log) and a “Ready for your report” page. It works in any PDF app and prints well.
  3. Fill it in over a few weeks. Some pages take five minutes; a week-long log takes a week. Each person fills in their own parts, about themselves.
  4. Bring it back, or type it in. Upload the filled PDF and the site shows you what it read and anything missing, so you can fix it. Or fill in the same steps right on the site. You can save a small backup file and pick up later.
  5. Read your report together, or on your own. On screen or as a PDF, at a calm time, with your batteries checked first.

What the report gives you

  • A plain-language summary of the whole picture, with headline scores and what they mean, strengths to protect, and the top few things to work on. It also says how complete your answers were, so you know how much weight to give it.
  • Findings for every workpaper, from your real numbers: who carries which kinds of work (including the invisible kind), jobs with no clear owner, each person’s battery and biggest stressors, patterns that keep coming back, and how steady your check-ins have been. Each section also says what it can’t tell you.
  • Connections across workpapers, like hours that look even while the mental load doesn’t, or tense days that line up with high-load days.
  • Things worth a second look: numbers that don’t add up, answers that seem to disagree, a missing week. Gently worded, because it may be a typo or it may be real.
  • Recommendations in order (now, this week, this month), each with the reason, a first step, words you could use, the right tool, and how you’ll know it’s working. Plus a 4 to 6 week plan built from them.
  • A kind view for each person, never a ranking. On the Individual road, a deeper look at your own wiring, patterns and conditions, plus a short way to explain yourself if you ever want to share it.
  • The Five Pillars view and a discussion guide with questions to talk through, ground rules for reading it together, and what to do if it lands hard. At the end, a short note on how the report works and a glossary.

It’s a fair read of how things are set up, never a verdict on anyone. It isn’t therapy and doesn’t diagnose. Everything stays on your device: the PDF is made, read and turned into a report right in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
