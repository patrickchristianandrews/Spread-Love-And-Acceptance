How it works

An outside lens on yourself

Simple versionRead the full page

The program gives you a calm view of yourself from the outside, like an honest auditor with no verdict on your worth. It looks at your situation, your wiring and what you discover.

  1. Your life situation changes how much you can hold.

    A new baby, a move, an illness or grief can shrink what you can carry before you notice. Seeing your load clearly lets you stop blaming yourself, or anyone else, for the season you're in.

    Dig deeper: your life situation

  2. Your wiring is information, not a flaw.

    Every brain handles stress, noise and conversation differently. Some people reset in minutes, others need hours. Knowing yours helps you explain it to the people around you.

    Dig deeper: your wiring

  3. Patterns show up when you write things down.

    Over a few weeks you may spot the time of day you get short, or the task that always slips. Seen from outside, they look like problems you can solve.

    Dig deeper: what you discover

  4. The static lives between two people.

    You say “in a minute”, meaning when you finish this. They hear sixty seconds. Nobody lied. Knowing your own signal well enough to say it out loud helps close the gap.

    Dig deeper: where the static comes from

  5. The technical parts are optional.

    Each idea comes first in everyday words. The science behind it is there if you want it. Skip it and the tools work just the same.

    Dig deeper: plain first, technical if you want it

  6. Repeating it is what makes it trustworthy.

    This is a practical guide, not a clinical tool, and your mood comes along when you fill it in. One entry is a mood. Twelve entries are a pattern.

    Dig deeper: an honest note
