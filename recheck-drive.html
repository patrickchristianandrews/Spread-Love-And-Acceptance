The Re-check Drive

A football drive where you can’t lose. Do the small, kind things from the complacency playbook for real: notice, ask, thank, share your side, ask for theirs. Each one moves the ball down the field toward a field goal. There’s no clock, and the Rut never scores.

Where this fits: This game puts Pillar V (“Notice the quiet incentives”) to work, with Pillar IV, Tune how you send and receive. In you: notice what you’ve stopped checking. Between you and others: give your context, ask for theirs, and re-check on a schedule, on the same team.

Who is this drive for?

Pick one person, or yourself. You’ll play it as the two of you against the Rut, the quiet drift that sets in when “it’s fine” stops being checked.
