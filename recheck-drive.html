    Why this works

    A football drive where you can’t lose. Do the small, kind things from the complacency playbook for real: notice, ask, thank, share your side, ask for theirs. Each one moves the ball down the field toward a field goal. There’s no clock, and the Rut never scores.

    How it works: Call a play, do it in real life, then tell the game you did. The ball moves only when you say so. Nobody checks but you, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your drive is saved only in this browser, and “Start over” erases it.
