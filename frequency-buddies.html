About the show

Gentle adventures

Storms, rope bridges, lost song pages and night walks, with a little suspense and a lot of heart. Nothing too scary, and every episode ends stronger than it started.

Made for watching together

About 15 minutes each, in short chapters you can jump between. Captions are always on, and the voices come from your own device. Turn them off anytime.

Quiet little lessons

Each story quietly shows one of the program’s five ideas, like checking your battery before you fix things, without a lecture in sight.

Your place in each episode is remembered in this browser only, so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent anywhere.
