About the show

Gentle adventures

Storms, rope bridges, lost song pages and night walks, with a little suspense and a lot of heart. Nothing too scary, and every episode ends stronger than it started. A short note before each episode says what happens, like a storm with thunder in episode 1. Lightning shows as a soft glow unless you turn bright flashes on.

Made for watching together

About 16 minutes each, in chapters of about 2 to 4 minutes. You can watch one chapter at a time and stop there. Captions are always on, and you can turn the voices and music off anytime.

Quiet little lessons

Each story quietly shows one of the program’s five ideas, like checking your battery before you fix things, without a lecture in sight.

Your place in each episode is remembered in this browser only, so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere.
