Watch on your TV

Press 📺 Watch on your TV under the picture. In Chrome on a computer or an Android phone, pick your Chromecast or Google TV (it needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as your device). On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, pick your Apple TV or AirPlay TV. The TV starts the episode from where you are. With a Chromecast, the TV keeps playing even if you close this page.

Season 1 and the trailer can be sent to a TV this way. Season 2 can’t yet, because its episodes are drawn live in your browser. For those, turn on screen mirroring on your phone, tablet or computer, then press Play.

About the show

Gentle adventures

Storms, rope bridges, lost song pages and night walks, with a little suspense and a lot of heart. Nothing too scary, and every episode ends stronger than it started. A short note before each episode says what happens, like a storm with thunder in episode 1. Lightning shows as a soft glow unless you turn bright flashes on.

Made for watching together

About 16 minutes each, in chapters of about 2 to 4 minutes. You can watch one chapter at a time and stop there. Captions are always on, and you can turn the voices and music off anytime. Every season has the same recorded voices for Tidbit, Sugarfoot and their friends. Watching with your grandkids? See the page for grandparents.

Quiet little lessons

Each story quietly shows one of the program’s five ideas, like checking your battery before you fix things, without a lecture in sight.

Your place in each episode is remembered in this browser only, so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere.
