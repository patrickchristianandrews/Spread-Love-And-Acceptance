What you type stays with you

Everything you type or choose in the tools, worksheets, check-ins and calculators on this site stays in your browser, on your own device. It is never collected: it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we never see it. Some tools, like Today’s Weather and the Carrier Wave Decoder, save your entries in your browser so you can come back to them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The only things that ever reach us are things you choose to send: your email address if you sign up, a message if you write to us or report a broken link, and entries you save in the optional household dashboard, which exists to keep them in sync for you and your household.

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use spreadloveandacceptance.com and The Objective Ledger™ (TOL-OS), operated by Patrick Christian Andrews. The public site is intentionally simple: it is a static website with no server-side code of its own. The one exception is the optional household dashboard, which uses sign-in accounts and a database hosted by Supabase (see Section 02). Almost everything described below happens through third-party services, not through code we control directly.

The site offers three levels of access, and each one involves a different amount of personal information:

  • Open Access (the free tools, printable summary, and preview chapters): no personal information is requested.
  • Free Member (the program-updates email list): your email address, held by Buttondown.
  • Paid Member (the paid Spread Love & Acceptance membership): your name, email, and payment details, held by Gumroad.

None of the three levels requires an account. Separately, anyone may choose to use the optional household dashboard, which does use an account. You sign in with a link sent to your email, never a password, and what you enter there is stored with Supabase so it syncs across your devices.

02

What Data Is Collected

Data touchpoints include:

  • Free membership (email list): if you join as a free member, the email address you enter is sent to and stored by Buttondown, our newsletter service. Buttondown may also collect standard email engagement data (such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked), depending on newsletter settings, governed by Buttondown's own privacy policy.
  • Paid membership data: if you subscribe to the paid membership, your name, email, and payment details are collected and processed entirely by Gumroad, our payment processor, not by us directly.
  • Hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses and request data as part of standard web server operation, governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Font loading: this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of the standard font-loading request, governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Household dashboard (optional): if you sign in to the dashboard, Supabase stores your email address (used to send sign-in links), the display name and household name you choose, and what you enter. Different entries are visible to different people:
    • Shared with your household partner: the weekly register (the Lemonade Stand hours), the ownership treaty (WP-03), weekly closing notes and saved Solvency Read scores, and your Battery & Stress Meter check-ins (WP-02).
    • Visible only to you: your Check Your State log. Your partner cannot see it, and database rules block them from reading it.
    Battery and state check-ins are self-reported wellbeing information, so please don't enter anything in a note that you wouldn't want stored. The dashboard keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.
  • Tools, worksheets and check-ins (never collected): everything you enter in the site’s tools, worksheets, check-ins and calculators, including Today’s Weather, the Lemonade Stand, the Signal Translator, the Carrier Wave Decoder, the Wiring Card, CALC-01 and the fill-in workpapers, is handled entirely in your browser. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party. Some tools keep your entries in your own browser so you can return to them; clearing your browser data removes them. Fill-in workpapers are saved only as PDFs on your own device. (The versions of these tools inside the household dashboard are different: what you enter there is saved, as described above.)
03

Third-Party Processors

We rely on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Gumroad — payment processing, subscription billing, receipt delivery, and content delivery for the paid membership.
  • Buttondown — email list and program-update delivery for free members.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery.
  • Supabase — sign-in, sign-in emails, and database hosting for the optional household dashboard.

We do not sell or share your personal data with any party beyond what is necessary for these services to function.

04

How Data Is Used

A free member's email address is used only to send program updates, and is never used for advertising or shared beyond Buttondown. Paid membership data is used solely to process your subscription, deliver content updates, and respond to support requests. Household dashboard entries are used only to show them back to you and, where noted above, to your household partner; we don't analyze them, and we look at them only if you ask us to help with a problem or the law requires it. We do not use your data for advertising.

05

Data Retention

Free members' email addresses are kept by Buttondown until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Payment and subscription records are retained by Gumroad according to their own retention policy and applicable financial recordkeeping requirements. We do not separately store a copy of your payment information.

Household dashboard data is kept until you delete it. Leaving a household removes you and your Battery & Stress Meter check-ins from it; the shared register, treaty, and closed weeks stay with the household for the remaining member, and are deleted once the last member leaves. Deleting your account from the dashboard also permanently erases your sign-in and your private state log. Deleted data may persist in Supabase's routine backups for a limited time before those backups are overwritten.

06

Your Rights

Free members can leave at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email. You may request details of what information is associated with your free or paid membership, request a copy of it, or request deletion (subject to what Gumroad, Buttondown, and applicable law require us to retain, such as records needed for tax or dispute purposes) by contacting us at the email in Section 09. Household dashboard users can also export their data as a file, or delete their account, directly from the dashboard at any time. Depending on your location, you may also have specific rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; this section will be expanded with jurisdiction-specific detail during attorney review.
