Production Roadmap

Carrier Wave Decoder — Phase II

12-week production plan for the next major content initiative. 18 audio recordings, 5 video lessons, new interactive tools.

Executive Summary

The Carrier Wave Decoder deepens the nervous system work started in the main framework. Phase I (current): written framework and interactive tools. Phase II (this plan): production of 18 audio-guided scenarios + 5 video lessons + supporting interactive tools, ready for integration into the member platform.

12-Week Production Timeline

Weeks 1-2: Planning & Scripts

Weeks starting Sept 23, Oct 1 | Owner: Strategy Lead + Audio Producer
✓ Finalize scenario list (6 scenarios × 3 state variations each = 18 recordings)
✓ Write scripts for all 18 recordings (~200-300 words each)
✓ Outline 5 video lessons (theory, practice, integration)
✓ Schedule recording sessions
✓ Source sound design elements (nature sounds, ambient audio)

Weeks 3-4: Audio Recording & Editing

Weeks starting Oct 8, Oct 15 | Owner: Audio Producer
✓ Record all 18 audio scenarios (4-5 per session)
✓ First pass editing (raw audio → podcast-ready)
✓ Add sound design layers (ambience, transitions, spacing)
✓ Quality check (consistency, EQ, dynamics)
→ Deliverable: 18 .mp3 files (finalized audio)

Weeks 5-7: Video Production

Weeks starting Oct 22, Oct 29, Nov 5 | Owner: Video Producer + Strategy Lead
✓ Shoot 5 video lessons (on-camera talking head or screen-based)
✓ Edit for YouTube/web (optimize for different platforms)
✓ Create motion graphics / supporting visuals
✓ Write video copy and CTAs
→ Deliverable: 5 .mp4 files + YouTube versions (embedded links)

Weeks 8-9: Interactive Tool Build

Weeks starting Nov 12, Nov 19 | Owner: Development + UX
✓ Build scenario selector UI (6 scenarios, choosable state)
✓ Integrate audio playback with interactive elements
✓ Add take-notes feature (optional)
✓ Create progress tracking (how many scenarios completed)
→ Deliverable: Carrier Wave Decoder interactive tool (live on staging)

Weeks 10-11: Integration & QA

Weeks starting Nov 26, Dec 3 | Owner: QA + Support
✓ Deploy to member platform (add to navigation, update help docs)
✓ Test on all devices/browsers
✓ Create trainer guide (how to explain this to members)
✓ Gather internal feedback from beta users
✓ Finalize launch emails & announcement copy

Week 12: Launch

Week starting Dec 10 | Owner: Product Lead
✓ Public launch announcement
✓ Update website (new section, new content links)
✓ Send launch email to current members
✓ Monitor support tickets (first 24 hours)
→ LIVE: Carrier Wave Decoder + 18 audio scenarios + 5 video lessons

Resource Requirements

  • Audio Producer: 4 weeks FTE (recording, editing, mastering)
  • Video Producer/Editor: 3 weeks FTE (shooting, editing, graphics)
  • Developer: 2 weeks FTE (interactive tool build)
  • UX/Design: 1 week (UI design, motion graphics)
  • Strategy Lead: 2 weeks (scripts, copy, integration)
  • QA: 1 week (testing, bug fixes)

Budget Estimate

  • Audio production: $8,000 (recording, editing, music licensing)
  • Video production: $6,000 (shooting, editing, graphics)
  • Development: $4,000 (tool build, integration)
  • Contingency (15%): $3,600
  • Total: ~$21,600

Success Metrics

  • All 18 audio recordings completed and polished (no technical issues)
  • 90%+ of beta users rate the audio quality as "excellent" or "good"
  • Average 60% completion rate (members finish at least 6+ scenarios)
  • NPS score for this feature ≥ 40
  • Launch on schedule (Week 12)

Risks & Contingencies

Risk: Recording delays → Contingency: Pre-record rough cuts in Week 1 to validate audio quality early

Risk: Video production underestimated → Contingency: Reduce to 3 video lessons (prioritize theory, beginner, advanced)

Risk: Tool build more complex than expected → Contingency: Launch without note-taking feature (add in Phase III)
