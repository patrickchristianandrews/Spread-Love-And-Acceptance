II. Phased Launch Strategy

PHASE 1: AWARENESS — "What is TOL-OS?"
Timeline: Weeks 1-2 (immediately after deployment)
Goal: Get curious people to the Diagnostic Snapshot
Channels: Email launch announcement, social media posts, maybe one guest post or interview
Key Message: "2 minutes to see where your relationship actually is — no judgment, no diagnosis, just clarity"
Success Metric: 500+ Snapshot completions (track via GA4 event)
PHASE 2: ENGAGEMENT — "Here's how it works"
Timeline: Weeks 3-6
Goal: Move Snapshot takers → Case Study readers → Playground users
Channels: Email sequence (5 emails over 3 weeks triggered by Snapshot completion), blog posts (one per week on specific TOL-OS topics)
Key Message: "See how real couples used this framework to move from burnout to sustainability"
Success Metric: 30%+ conversion from Snapshot → Case Study; 20%+ Click-through to Playground
PHASE 3: CONVERSION — "Ready to go deeper?"
Timeline: Weeks 7-12
Goal: Convert engaged users → Survival Kit membership
Channels: Retargeting ads (YouTube, Facebook) to Snapshot/Case Study viewers, email nurture sequence for Playground users, maybe a free mini-course
Key Message: "The Survival Kit is everything above, plus 6 more workpapers, video training, and a 12-week curriculum. Members get everything as it ships."
Success Metric: 50+ new Survival Kit memberships; $2,000+ new MRR
PHASE 4: DEEPENING — "What's next?"
Timeline: Weeks 13-16 (overlaps with Carrier Wave Decoder launch)
Goal: Launch Phase II content (Carrier Wave Decoder) to existing members
Channels: Member-only email, new reference section on website, maybe a launch webinar
Key Message: "18 audio scenarios, 5 video lessons, go deeper into nervous system work"
Success Metric: 80%+ of members try at least one Decoder scenario within 2 weeks of launch

III. Marketing Angles (by Traffic Source)

Organic Search: "How to divide household work fairly," "relationship workload stress," "why do I resent my partner," "couples communication tools" → these searches land on Snapshot or content hub pages, not product pages

Email (owned list): Current subscribers get announced new content first. Use "first look" language for early-access feel.

Social Media: Focus on: before/after quotes from case studies, bite-sized nervous system facts, "3 things you're probably invisible-doing" lists (thread content)

Paid Ads: Retarget Snapshot viewers who didn't convert within 30 days. Message: "87% of people who took the snapshot wanted to try the tools. Here's the Playground."

Referral/Organic: Build in "share your result" feature on Snapshot (optional). People often share assessment results naturally.
