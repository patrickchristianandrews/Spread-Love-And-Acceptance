The self path is for you, on your own. No partner, family or team needed. It helps you see what you’re carrying, notice your own rhythms and reactions, and find kind words for them, so that everything else, including how you get along with others, gets a little easier.

Coming here after a loss? Go as slowly as you need. You can take one step at a time, or skip any that don’t feel right yet. Grief and later life is there whenever you want to go back.

Why start with yourself

Every conversation you have runs through your own state first. When your battery is low, the same words land harder. When you know how you’re wired and what settles you, you can ask for what helps instead of hoping people guess. That’s Pillar III, Read your state first, and Pillar IV, Tune how you send and receive, starting inside you.

Self-discovery isn’t self-criticism. The goal is to understand yourself as kindly as you’d want to be understood.

A gentle first week

  • Every morning: one minute with your battery or Today’s Weather.
  • Once this week: read Know your own wiring and make your Wiring Card.
  • Midweek: write six lines of the rulebook from the home you grew up in, and circle one rule you’d like to change.
  • Before the weekend: fill in your Calm-Down Kit.
  • Whenever something stings: try fact, feeling and ask.
