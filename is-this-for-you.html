Before you start

Is this right for you?

Simple versionRead the full page

The Objective Ledger helps you understand your own stress and reactions, then gives two people a fair way to see what each of you carries. This page helps you decide if it fits.

  1. It's a shared ledger, not a scorecard.

    Each person fills in worksheets about themselves, then you read them together. It isn't therapy, a diagnosis, or a way to prove who's right. Every score describes the arrangement, never a person.

    Dig deeper: what it is and isn't

  2. It fits when the same arguments keep coming back.

    It helps when someone carries work the other can't see, and both of you will write down your own side honestly. You can also start on your own.

    Dig deeper: who it's for

  3. Safety comes before any worksheet.

    If someone involved is in crisis, or afraid of the other person, this isn't the right tool right now. In the US, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233.

    Dig deeper: where to get help

  4. Right now, all of it is free.

    The program is still being written. Some pages open with no sign-up, and the rest open once you enter your email. Nothing is charged now.

    Dig deeper: ways in

  5. Every page is listed in one place.

    The full directory groups every live page by what it's for: the book, the worksheets, the tools and more.

    Dig deeper: where everything is
