Articles worth your time

A hand-picked library of articles that go with this program: some help you understand yourself, and some help you get along with the people you share time, space, work or care with.

They come from sources we trust: Psychology Today, Greater Good Magazine from UC Berkeley, The Gottman Institute, Harvard Health, ADDitude, CHADD and the National Autistic Society. Each one is sorted by topic and by the Five Pillars it belongs to. The one-line summaries are ours; the articles open on the publishers’ sites in a new tab.

New: you can also ask Professor Puddles for an article on any topic, such as “find me an article about stress”, and he’ll pick three good ones.

Nothing you do on this page is sent anywhere. The only thing kept is a short list of the articles you’ve opened, in this browser, so new picks can skip them.
