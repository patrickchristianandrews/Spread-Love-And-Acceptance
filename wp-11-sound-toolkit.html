WP-11 Companion — Sound Toolkit

These are plain sound generators — a steady bass tone, a steady click pattern, and brown noise. Some people find one or more of these calming as part of a personal sensory toolkit; others won't, and that's useful information too. Nothing here is a validated mechanism for stress relief or nervous-system regulation — see WP-11's "Note on the Science" for the full context.

Try one at a time, at a comfortable volume, and notice how it actually feels for you.

Steady Bass Tone (40 Hz) A low, continuous hum some people describe as physically grounding.
Steady Pulse (40 Hz Click Pattern) An evenly repeating pulse — optional, some people enjoy it, no specific effect is established.
Brown Noise A smooth, low-pitched static that can mask irregular background sounds.

Start at low volume, especially with headphones — bass tones can feel more intense than expected.
