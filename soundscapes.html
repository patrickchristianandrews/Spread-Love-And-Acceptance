Soundscape Catalog

Every track below has actually been produced and is ready to hear. This page grows one real track at a time, not from a planned track list.

A note on what these are: these are ambient, atmospheric audio pieces, not a validated therapeutic or clinical tool. Some people find ambient and low-frequency audio calming as part of a personal listening habit. That's a matter of personal taste, not an established treatment.

What this page is, and isn't. This catalog holds functional and ambient audio, plus one grounding song. No workpaper requires audio, and none ever will. Any pairing below is only a suggestion for people who like to listen.

🎧 Soundscapes sound best with headphones. The deep, low tones and the gentle left-to-right movement are much of what makes them calming, and small speakers can’t carry them.

1. The Breath Beneath

40 Hz · 80 BPM · Vibroacoustic ambient · 2:55

The design. A pure 40 Hz drone you can almost feel, under an 80 BPM rhythm that matches the pace of a steady, resting breath.

How it feels. The low tone sits deep and heavy, a little like a weighted blanket for the ears. It comes through best on good over-ear headphones.

Good for. A bridge out of stressful or intensely analytical work: a few minutes to reset before you walk in the door, talk with family or settle into the evening.

2. One Breath to Anchor You

40 Hz · 75 BPM · Minimal ambient with guided voice · 2:55

The design. Very spare and spacious. The tempo drops to 75 BPM, with long quiet spaces between the guided words.

How it feels. Bright, busy sounds are stripped away, and a wide, soft reverb makes it feel like sitting alone in a large, calm room.

Good for. Shutting the world out for three minutes, when the room is too loud or the lights too bright and you need to find your baseline again.

3. Nothing Needs to Change

40 Hz · 65 BPM · Deep ambient · 2:56

The design. The slowest and deepest piece so far, at 65 BPM, made with dark, woody tones that sound more like strings than anything digital.

How it feels. The pace sits a little below a typical resting heartbeat. Many people find their breathing slows along with the long, slow swells.

Good for. The moment frustration or stress is spiking and you want something steady to hold on to. It pairs well with the Calm-Down Kit (WP-11), if you like to listen while you use it.

4. The Road We Made

40 Hz pulse · Americana · 2:49

The design. A song rather than an ambient piece: acoustic guitars and vocals in an outlaw-country style, with a quiet 40 Hz pulse underneath.

How it feels. Steady and grounded, a rhythm to walk with rather than a stillness to sink into.

Good for. Feeling anchored by where you come from. It honors a much-loved legacy and the roots of a childhood, and it’s a reminder of resilience, of endurance, and of the history you carry with you.

Walking companions. This is Tidbit and Sugarfoot’s song, a road for two best friends to walk side by side, the way they do in the Frequency Journey.
