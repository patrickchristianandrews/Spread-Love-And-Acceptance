Brain Breakers

Come get your brain wrecked. Broken in a good way. Three instrumental pieces for a short break, each with a different job: one to quiet a busy mind, one to give a flat afternoon a spark, one to get you moving again. Not sure which to press? find your Brain Breaker. Then see and feel it.

Music by Christian’s Lab. © 2026 Christian’s Lab.

🎧 Headphones help, and start with the volume low. These are meant to be felt as well as heard. Shooting Star is the gentle one; the other two have bigger moments.

1. Shooting Star

math-music · chill · 3:27

The softest and most floating track in the set. The mathematical complexity is still there, but it glides rather than pushes. Very airy and low-energy.

Mind
Strongly quiets mental noise and looping thoughts. A gentle, floating daydream state with very little cognitive demand.
Body
Noticeably lowers arousal: a slower heart rate and deeper, easier breathing. One of the most physically calming of the three.
Best for
Mild anxiety, restlessness, or pre-sleep quieting when you want to wind down without fully shutting off.

2. Thunderous Shimmer

math-music · experimental · acoustic · 2:51

The most textural and restless track. Experimental acoustic elements, sudden shimmering moments and the occasional thunderous low end make it dynamic and unpredictable.

Mind
Stimulates curiosity and mild alertness. The shifting textures keep the mind from going completely flat or foggy.
Body
A subtle physical “aliveness”: a little more body awareness and breathing depth, without raising stress.
Best for
Recovery periods when pure calm feels too passive. Gentle activation while you still decompress.

3. Watching a Shooting Star

avant-garde · math-rock · orchestral · 3:38

The most expansive and cinematic piece. Math-rock rhythmic drive meets full orchestral writing, with an adventurous, journey-like arc.

Mind
A sense of expansion and forward motion. It can lift mood and bring mild exhilaration or imaginative openness.
Body
Keeps a relaxed baseline and adds a light energetic current. Feels purposeful rather than heavy or draining.
Best for
Decompression that still feels productive: walking, light planning, or shifting out of a stuck or low-energy mental state.
