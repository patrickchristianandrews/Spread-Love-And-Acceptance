How to Use These Soundscapes

  • Don't require them. These are tools for people who find them helpful, not obligations. Silence is always acceptable.
  • Use intentionally. Rather than background music all day, play a soundscape for 5-15 minutes before or during the specific moment it's designed for.
  • Listen solo first. Try a soundscape alone before using it in a shared moment, so you know how it lands for you.
  • Volume matters. Low volume (barely audible) often works better than background-music level. You're creating a held space, not filling silence.
  • Stop if it doesn't help. Not every person finds ambient audio settling. If a soundscape makes something harder rather than easier, stop and don't use it.
  • Pair with specific moments. These work best when anchored to an actual conversation, decision point, or transition — not as a default state.

Soundscapes & The Workpaper Suite

Soundscapes are optional and supplemental. No workpaper requires audio, no calculator depends on it, and no chapter references a soundscape as necessary. They exist in parallel to the framework: helpful for some households, unneeded for others, and completely optional as part of the implementation.
