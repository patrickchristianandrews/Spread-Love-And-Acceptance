Email updates

Everything is open

Everything is open while the site is being built. If you’d like, leave your email to hear about new chapters and tools. There’s no sign-up to use anything here, nothing is locked, and nothing is charged.

Not sure it’s for you? See if this is right for you. For what is open and what is shared, see Ways in.

How the email updates work

  1. Enter your email above. It’s optional, and every page is open whether you do or not.
  2. Your address goes to Buttondown, our newsletter service. They may send a confirmation email. Confirming it keeps you on the update list.
  3. You’ll get a short note when a new chapter, workpaper or tool goes live. That’s all.

This site doesn’t store your email on its own server. Your browser remembers that you signed up, and clearing your browser data clears that.
