What happens

  1. 0:00 Curtain up. The marquee lights up, the stage rises out of the floor, the curtains open, and the pals ride up on two little lifts in top hats and bow ties.
  2. 0:04 Tune in! Side steps together, a spin and a jump, while the Moon sings along in a starry sky.
  3. 0:09 Tidbit’s verse. A puff of sparkles, and Tidbit is a rock star in shades. She zooms across the stage with the ducklings following the leader, and when the sky goes gray, a rainbow comes out.
  4. 0:17 Sugarfoot’s verse. Flower crowns and a sunset. Sugarfoot takes her time across the stage with Dot the snail beside her, and when it gets too loud, the lights go soft while she takes a breath.
  5. 0:24 Different is okay. Capes and crowns, rainbow lights, and a mirror dance. The band rises up at the back: Beep on drums, Hopper on keys, Ollie on horn and Nutmeg on guitar, with Professor Puddles conducting.
  6. 0:31 The big chorus. Sparkly vests, a disco ball, confetti cannons, a heart made of lights, and a conga line all the way around the stage.
  7. 0:52 Into space. Behind the curtain, space suits. The stage splits in two: Tidbit goes fast, Sugarfoot floats slow, one says yes and one says “No, thank you,” and then they glide back together on a turntable to say sorry.
  8. 1:04 Runnin’ free. Pirate hats, a sunset sea, a belly slide across the whole stage, and everyone running round together.
  9. 1:10 Grand finale. Marching band uniforms for everyone, a starburst, fireworks, a parade around the stage, and a lift that raises the pals up high for the last note.
  10. 1:27 Take a bow. Everyone bows, the crowd cheers, and the stage sinks away as the curtain closes.

Who’s in it

Tidbit and Sugarfoot

The stars, singing their own verses and every chorus, with eight costume changes.

The Buddies Band

Beep the robot on drums, Hopper the frog on keys, Ollie the owl on horn and Nutmeg the squirrel on guitar, with Professor Puddles conducting.

The chorus line and friends

The ducklings, Dot the snail, the butterfly and the Moon, singing along, plus a whole crowd with glow sticks and “Go pals!” signs.
