A calm place to wander into

The Bears Dojo

This is a quiet temple garden. Tidbit and Sugarfoot tend the grounds, slowly, and you are welcome to just watch. Build a little bear that looks like you, then step through the doors into a warm room where something gentle waits: a breath, a bell, a card, a stone to stack. It is always something different, one thing at a time, with no timers, no scores and no way to lose. There is a music corner inside too, with instruments to play and long, slow songs to sit with. Everything you type or choose stays in this browser, on your device. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere, and sound stays off until you turn it on.
