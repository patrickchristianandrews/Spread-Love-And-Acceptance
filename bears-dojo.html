A calm place to wander into

The Bears Dojo

This is a quiet temple garden. Tidbit and Sugarfoot tend the grounds, slowly, and you are welcome to just watch. Build a little bear that looks like you, then step through the doors into a warm room where something gentle waits: a breath, a bell, a card, a stone to stack. It is always something different, one thing at a time, with no timers, no scores and no way to lose. Everything you type or choose stays in this browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and sound stays off until you turn it on.

Build a bear who looks the way you like. They will wait on the grounds by the gate and sit with you inside. Your choices and the name are kept only in this browser, on this device.

Inside the dojo

Nothing here is scored or timed. If you turn drifting on, a new offering arrives every little while, but never while you are in the middle of something.
