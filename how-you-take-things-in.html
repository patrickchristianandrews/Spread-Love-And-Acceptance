How you take things in: make the site fit your mind

Minds take things in differently. Some people want the short steps first. Some want words that mean exactly what they say. Some would rather listen than read. Pick what fits you, and every page on this site adapts.

  • Your wiring is information, not a flaw. Knowing how you take things in is useful, the way knowing your shoe size is useful.
  • People vary within every neurotype. Two autistic people, or two people with ADHD, can want quite different things. So each choice shows exactly what it turns on, and you can untick any of it.
  • It’s optional, and no labels are needed. Pick a neurotype, pick plain preferences, or both. Change it any time, or clear it all with one button.
  • It stays on this device. Your choices are kept in one small setting in this browser and never sent anywhere. What’s stored on this device
