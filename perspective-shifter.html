The Perspective Shifter: see it from their side

Two people can share the same moment and still see two different things. The Perspective Shifter helps you step round to the other person’s side: who they are, how they’re wired, the state they were in, what was going on around them, and, most often missed, what they could see that you couldn’t.

How it works: Tap what might be true for them. Their side of the moment builds itself as you go, with questions to check instead of guessing and a sentence to bridge the gap. It never decides who was right. It shows how each of you could be seeing something true.

Everything happens on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere or kept.

Why what you can see changes what’s true for you

Most misunderstandings aren’t about different values. They’re about different information. One person saw the message from school; the other didn’t. One person knew the deadline; the other only heard the hurry. Each fills the gaps with what they can see, and the result can look like carelessness, nagging or coldness, when it’s really two people working from two different pictures.

That’s why the Shifter asks three questions about visibility: what only they could see, what only you could see, and what neither of you could. The answers often explain the whole moment before anyone needs to apologize.
