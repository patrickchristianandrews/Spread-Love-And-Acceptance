Today's Weather

People have inner weather. Some mornings are clear, some are gusty, some are fogged in, and it changes without asking your permission. This is a one-minute reading of today's conditions: the sky, the pressure, the ground underneath, and what's blowing in.

Weather is the point of the metaphor. It isn't a grade, a diagnosis or a verdict on you, any more than rain is a verdict on a town. Conditions pass. Knowing them just tells you what today is good for, and what to leave until the front moves through.
