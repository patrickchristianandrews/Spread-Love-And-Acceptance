The Nervous System Ladder

Polyvagal theory describes three broad states, arranged like rungs on a ladder. Pick the one closest to true right now. This is a check on yourself, not a reading of anyone else. If you are not on the top rung, do not start a hard talk. A sharper voice, a repeated sentence, or the urge to add a second topic are early signs you are already leaving the conversation. Stop there. The signs, and how to regroup, are on Check-ins. The order for when you are ready is on Check-ins. The Signal Translator is for the sentence, after the body can hear it.

Breath is easy, your shoulders aren’t braced, and you can hear feedback without immediately defending yourself. This is the state the rest of the site actually works in.
