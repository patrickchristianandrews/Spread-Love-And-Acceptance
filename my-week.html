Your week in weather

Tap the weather each day turned out to be. See your week as a little weather report, then play with next week’s forecast and watch it change as you change the plan.

How the forecast works. It starts from your own recent days, adds or subtracts a fixed number of points for each change you make, and shows every step in a “why” panel. It is a playful planning tool built only from what you enter, not a prediction and not a diagnosis.

Already using Today’s Weather? Those days fill in here on their own, and nothing about them changes.

1. Capture your week

For each day, tap the weather that fits it best. That is all you need. Open “More about this day” if you want to add energy, stress, sleep, connection, what happened, or a note.

2. Your weather report

A recap of the week on screen, written by a few fixed rules on your device. It points out what stood out as possibilities, never as conclusions.

3. Next week’s forecast

Change what you expect next week and watch the days move. The shaded band is how sure the forecast is: it widens when the plan has big unknowns and narrows when it is steady.

Model adjusters

Everything starts at “about usual”. Move anything that will be different next week.

      What this page keeps

      Your days, notes, saved weeks, pinned plans and reminder choice are kept in this browser on this device, together under one entry. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. Days you saved in Today’s Weather are read, never changed. Clearing your browser data removes everything here, and the buttons above remove it on purpose. See all of it on What’s on this device.

      This is a reflection and planning tool, not a diagnosis or a prediction. Weather words describe days, not people.
