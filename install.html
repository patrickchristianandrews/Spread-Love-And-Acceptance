Put the app on your phone

Put the whole site on your Android phone as an app, the same one Chrome installs when you choose Install app. It gets its own icon, opens full screen with no address bar, and keeps working when your signal drops.

Checking your phone…

One tap if your browser allows it. Steps below if not.

What you get

  • An icon on your home screen and in your app drawer, like any other app.
  • Full screen, with no browser bars in the way.
  • The games, tools, chat and cartoons keep working without a connection once you have opened them.
  • Press and hold the icon for quick jumps: Today’s Weather, Upset right now?, Share the load and the Night Garden.
  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere, the same as on the website. It stays on your phone.
  • No store account, no download size to worry about, and updates arrive by themselves.

Install it with Chrome on Android

  1. Open spreadloveandacceptance.com in Chrome. Use a normal tab, not an Incognito one.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right of Chrome.
  3. Tap Install app. If you only see Add to Home screen, tap that and then choose Install.
  4. Tap Install once more to confirm. The icon with the two little bubbles lands on your home screen.
  5. Open it from there. It opens full screen, like an app you downloaded.

If the button at the top of this page is showing, you can tap it instead of doing steps 2 to 4.

Another browser?

  • Samsung Internet: tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen. Newer versions also show an install icon in the address bar.
  • Firefox: tap the three dots, then Install.
  • Edge: tap the three dots, then Add to phone.
  • iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • A computer: Chrome and Edge show an install icon at the right of the address bar.

Can it have widgets?

Not yet, and here is the plain reason. A real Android widget, the kind that sits on your home screen and updates itself, has to be built into a downloaded Android app. An app that Chrome installs from a website can’t add one, and the standard for web widgets only works on Windows right now. We would rather tell you that than fake it.

Two things get you close today:

  • Press and hold the app icon. A short list of quick jumps pops up, and you can drag any of them out onto your home screen as its own icon.
  • Pin a single page. Open one of these in Chrome, then use the menu and Add to Home screen. You get a one-tap tile for just that page.
  • The Night Garden
  • Today’s Weather
  • Today’s small moment
  • Ask Professor Puddles
  • Pal Cam TV

A true Android widget, such as a daily small moment or a one-tap Breathe, would mean building a native Android app that wraps this site. That is a bigger project, and it is on the idea list.

If the install option doesn’t show

  • Update Chrome from the Play Store. Install needs a fairly recent version.
  • Make sure you are on spreadloveandacceptance.com itself, not inside another app’s built-in browser. Tap the three dots and choose Open in Chrome.
  • Stay on the site for a few seconds first. Chrome sometimes waits a moment before it offers to install.
  • Already installed? Look in your app drawer for The Ledger. Chrome only offers Install once.
  • To remove it later, press and hold the icon and choose Uninstall. Anything saved in it stays only on your phone.

Try this: Once it is on your home screen, open it and try Start in 10 minutes. It is a good first thing to do with a new app.
