Install it with Chrome on an Android phone or tablet

  1. Open spreadloveandacceptance.com in Chrome. Use a normal tab, not an Incognito one.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right of Chrome.
  3. Tap Install app. If you only see Add to Home screen, tap that and then choose Install.
  4. Tap Install once more to confirm. The icon with the two little bubbles lands on your home screen.
  5. Open it from there. It opens full screen, like an app you downloaded.

If the button above is showing, you can tap it instead of doing steps 2 to 4.

Another browser on Android?

  • Samsung Internet: tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen. Newer versions also show an install icon in the address bar.
  • Firefox: tap the three dots, then Install.
  • Edge: tap the three dots, then Add to phone.

If the install option doesn’t show

  • Update Chrome from the Play Store. Install needs a fairly recent version.
  • Make sure you are on spreadloveandacceptance.com itself, not inside another app’s built-in browser. Tap the three dots and choose Open in Chrome.
  • Stay on the site for a few seconds first. Chrome sometimes waits a moment before it offers to install.
  • Already installed? Look in your app drawer for The Ledger. Chrome only offers Install once.
  • To remove it later, press and hold the icon and choose Uninstall. Anything saved in it stays only on your phone.

On your iPhone or iPad today

You do not have to wait. The whole site already works from your Home Screen, the same site in its own icon with no browser bars.

  1. Open spreadloveandacceptance.com in Safari. (Other iPhone browsers can’t add it, so switch to Safari if you are in one.)
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon with the two little bubbles lands on your Home Screen.
  5. Open it from there. It opens full screen.

One honest difference: iPhones don’t let a web page make the phone vibrate, so the “feel the sound” buzz only works on Android. You can still see and hear everything.

Install it on Windows 10 or 11

It installs as a web app: its own window and icon, no store and no download page. Use Edge or Chrome.

  1. Open spreadloveandacceptance.com in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for the small install icon (a screen with a down arrow, or a plus). Click it.
  3. No icon? Open the menu (the three dots at the top right). In Edge choose Apps, then Install this site as an app. In Chrome choose Cast, save and share, then Install page as app.
  4. Click Install. If it offers, tick Pin to taskbar and Pin to Start.
  5. It opens in its own window. Find it any time in the Start menu under The Ledger.

It keeps working without a connection once you have opened pages, and updates by itself. To remove it later, open the app, click the three dots in its title bar and choose Uninstall, or use Settings, then Apps.

Firefox on Windows doesn’t install web apps, but the site works fine in a normal tab. A Microsoft Store version and Windows widgets are not built yet.

On a Mac or Linux computer

  1. Open spreadloveandacceptance.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow).
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window and gets its own icon.

Safari on a Mac: choose File, then Add to Dock. Firefox doesn’t install web apps, but the site works fine in a normal tab.

What you get

  • An icon on your home screen, like any other app.
  • Full screen, with no browser bars in the way.
  • The games, tools, chat and cartoons keep working without a connection once you have opened them.
  • On Android, press and hold the icon for quick jumps: Today’s Weather, Upset right now?, Share the load and the Night Garden.
  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere, the same as on the website. It stays on your phone.
  • No store account, and updates arrive by themselves. Once installed, the app saves an offline copy of about 7 MB, once (the website alone saves almost nothing until you open a page).

Can it have widgets?

Not yet, and here is the plain reason. A real Android widget, the kind that sits on your home screen and updates itself, has to be built into a downloaded Android app. An app that Chrome installs from a website can’t add one, and the standard for web widgets only works on Windows right now. We would rather tell you that than fake it.

Two things get you close today:

  • Press and hold the app icon (Android). A short list of quick jumps pops up, and you can drag any of them out onto your home screen as its own icon.
  • Pin a single page. Open one of these, then use the browser menu and Add to Home screen. You get a one-tap tile for just that page.
  • The Night Garden
  • Today’s Weather
  • Today’s small moment
  • Ask Professor Puddles
  • Pal Cam TV

A true widget, such as a daily small moment or a one-tap Breathe, would mean building a native app that wraps this site. That is a bigger project, and it is on the idea list.

Try this: Once it is on your home screen, open it and try Start in 10 minutes. It is a good first thing to do with a new app.
