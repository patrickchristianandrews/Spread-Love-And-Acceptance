The Lemonade Stand

When life hands you chores, split the lemons.

One stand, two to eight people. List what it took to keep your place running this week, and how many hours each of you put in. If you like, add the money side too: rent, bills, shared shopping. The split shows up as a plain fact, not a verdict on anyone.

The Lemonade Stand puts Pillar I, See the whole load to work, with a nod to Pillar V, Notice the quiet incentives. In you: you count the hours you really put in, including the quiet jobs. Between you and others: you all see the same picture, and can spot work that has quietly drifted to one person.

Who's at the stand? Tap a name to change it.

The starting numbers are just an example.

One line per job. Put the hours each person spent on it this week.

One line per shared cost, like rent, power, internet or groceries. Put what each person actually paid. Leave this empty if money isn't part of your picture.

Add some hours above to see how the work is split.

How to use it: Each person fills in their own hours, ideally from a week of the “who did what” log. Then look at it together. Copy the result into your group chat if that helps. The totals also go into CALC-01, Can the load last?
