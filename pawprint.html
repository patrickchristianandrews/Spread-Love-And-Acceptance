Know yourself

Your Pawprint: a personal statement, made easy

Everyone leaves their own pawprint. This one is a short, warm note about you: how you’re wired, what fills and drains your battery, how you show care, and what helps when you’re stretched. Nine easy steps, mostly tapping.

How it works: Pick what fits in each step, and add a line in your own words if you like. Your Pawprint writes itself as you go, and Things you might not have noticed shows connections between your choices that are easy to miss, and why they matter to the people around you.

Everything happens on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and a draft is kept only if you choose.

Sharing your Pawprint

  • Pick a calm moment. Share it as information, not a list of rules: “I wrote this so you don’t have to guess. Would you read it?”
  • Invite theirs. Two Pawprints side by side show where you’re alike and where you differ, and nobody has to be wrong.
  • Update it. Seasons change, and so does what you need. Come back when life shifts.
