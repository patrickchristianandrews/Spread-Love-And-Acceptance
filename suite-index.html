The official list of what’s built today. If something is listed here, it’s live and ready to use. If it isn’t listed, it isn’t live yet. Looking for where to begin? See which part of the program to use.

  1. Some of it is free for anyone.

    The Preface and Chapters I and II, the Lemonade Stand, Today’s Weather, the Wiring Card, the Signal Translator, the Carrier Wave Decoder, the Wired Differently, Check-ins, Turning Toward and Complacency guides, the Program Overview and a printable infographic. Also free: the Five Pillars, Know your own wiring, the Re-check Drive, the Professor’s Library, the long form of “Is the setup working for everyone?” (CALC-01) for 2–8 people, the articles page, the quest map, Drift and the calm games. Each has its own page on the site.

    Dig deeper: the free, public list

  2. The book has a preface and five chapters so far.

    Each one has its own page on the site. All of them are free to read, with no sign-up. They are also collected in one PDF bundle.

    Dig deeper: each chapter and what it covers

  3. Seven workpapers are live.

    Workpapers are worksheets you fill in: WP-01, 02, 03, 04, 09, 11 and 13. All of them are free and open. The numbers have gaps on purpose, because they are slots in a bigger plan. Fill any of them in on screen or as a fillable PDF, or do them all in the Workpaper Suite, in the order that fits your relationship.

    Dig deeper: each workpaper and when to use it

  4. Use them in order, not all at once.

    WP-01, then WP-02, WP-03, WP-09 and WP-13, with WP-04 once a month and WP-11 whenever either of you is too wound up to talk. If you are new, start with WP-01 and keep one week of it before touching anything else.

    Dig deeper: the order and where to start

  5. One calculator, one record, one program.

    Is the setup working for everyone? (CALC-01) gives a single read on whether the way you split the work can last. REPORT-01 keeps your weekly record. PROG-01 walks you through six weeks.

    Dig deeper: the calculator and the program

  6. Some things aren’t built yet.

    Chapters VI through XII, the remaining workpapers, and CALC-02 through CALC-07 are not yet written. New things are added to the list the day they go live.
