The official list of what’s built today. If something is listed here, it’s live and ready to use. If it isn’t listed, it isn’t live yet.

    Chapters I and II, the Lemonade Stand and the Nervous System Ladder, the Wiring Card, the Signal Translator, the Wired Differently and Check-ins guides, the Program Overview and a printable infographic. Each has its own page on the site.

    Workpapers are worksheets you fill in: WP-01, 02, 03, 04, 09, 11 and 13. The numbers have gaps on purpose, because they are slots in a bigger plan.

    CALC-01 gives a single read on whether the way you split the work looks sustainable. REPORT-01 keeps your weekly record. PROG-01 walks you through six weeks.
