Orientation

Start Here

Every instrument in the Suite works on its own, but they were built to be picked up in an order. Used all at once they collapse into paperwork, which is the most common way a household abandons the whole thing in week two.

If you're new: begin with WP-01 and keep a single week of the Field Audit before touching anything else. One week of honest observation is what makes every other instrument here readable. Nothing downstream works without it.

If something is on fire right now: WP-02 first, then WP-09. Check what you're carrying before you decide what the conversation is about.

If the same argument keeps recurring: that's a WP-03 problem, not a communication problem. Unowned work defaults to whoever notices first, every single time.

The intended sequence runs WP-01 → WP-02 → WP-03 → WP-09 → WP-13, with CALC-01 read once the first three have produced numbers, and WP-04 picked up monthly after that. The six-week program that paces this is drafted but not yet published; see Not Yet Built.

The Workpapers

Six built. The numbering is not sequential and never has been — the numbers are slots in a planned framework, and the gaps are honest rather than accidental.

WP-01

Field Audit & Neutral Refusals

Live page
What it does
A seven-day raw log of what was actually done, by whom, for roughly how long — kept before any interpretation is added. Part B adds scripts for declining or deferring a request without it becoming a fight.
When to use
First, always. Then one full week per quarter as a re-baseline. Not continuously — permanent logging turns a diagnostic into a chore, which is where this all started.
Feeds
Workload Balance (WB) into CALC-01.

WP-02

Autonomic Saturation Index

Live page
What it does
Five factors, rated 0–4 by each person about themselves, separating "how much am I already carrying" from "how upset am I about this specific thing."
When to use
Daily as a sixty-second habit, and always before a conversation you expect to be hard. Never filled in about the other person.
Feeds
Autonomic Saturation (AS) into CALC-01, entering inverted as 1 − AS.

WP-03

Domestic RACI Treaty

Live page
What it does
Assigns exactly one Responsible and one Accountable to every recurring task, so ownership stops getting renegotiated by default every week.
When to use
After two weeks of Field Audit data, so you're assigning from evidence rather than impressions. Amend it whenever life changes — it's a living agreement, and one that's never been amended probably isn't being used.
Feeds
Ownership Clarity (OC) into CALC-01.

Unbilled Deficit Audit

Live page
What it does
A monthly pass across four weeks of Field Audits to find the tasks that keep becoming deficits, and to classify each as a structural gap, a capacity issue, or a one-off.
When to use
Monthly, not weekly. It exists specifically for patterns that are invisible at the weekly scale.
Feeds
Nothing scored. It drives amendments to WP-03.

Tone Transducer & Filter

Live page

Two directions. The transducer converts your raw reaction into fact, feeling, and ask before you send it. The filter decides how much weight to give what you just received.

In the moment, on one thing that actually stung. Running everything through it turns a repair tool into a performance.

Phase-Locked Loop Protocol

A ninety-second daily check-in — load, friction, ask — with no debate, no solving, and no rebuttal. Small continuous corrections instead of occasional large ones.

CALC-01

The Solvency Read

Live page
What it does
Takes the numbers the workpapers produce and returns a single 0–1 read on whether the current workload arrangement looks sustainable. It scores the arrangement — not either person, and not the relationship.
When to use
At the weekly closing, once WP-01, WP-02 and WP-03 have each produced a figure. A single read describes one week; the trend is what tells you whether anything is moving.
Takes
WB from WP-01, OC from WP-03, AS from WP-02, and optionally RF from WP-09.
File
/workpapers/calculators/calc01-solvency.html

The calculator is browser-based and isn't reproduced as a static page in the bundle. It runs locally and stores nothing.

Architecture

How They Connect

Four workpapers produce numbers. Two workpapers don't, and that's deliberate — not everything worth doing is worth scoring.

SourceProducesDefinitionUsed in
WP-01WB Workload Balance — 1 − |pctA − pctB| ÷ 100 Both scores
WP-03OC Ownership Clarity — tasks with both R and A, over total tasks Both scores
WP-02AS Autonomic Saturation — mean of both partners' five-factor scores Both scores
WP-09RF Retuning Frequency — retunes used over friction events Apex only
WP-04— Monthly deficit classification; drives treaty amendments Not scored
WP-13— Daily qualitative notes; repeated items promote to WP-03 or WP-09 Not scored

P(Solvency) uses WB, OC and AS, and asks the narrow question: is this week's workload sustainable as structured? The apex score adds RF and asks the wider one: is the whole system working, including repair? They can disagree, and the disagreement is the useful part — a household can have a well-shared, clearly-owned workload and still not be repairing after friction.

What none of this will ever take as an input: tone of voice, recordings, message sentiment, response timestamps, or anything describing one person without their direct participation in entering it. Every figure here comes from shared, observable things that both people enter about their own week.

No score on this site is ever computed from one partner's inputs alone, and none of it is a validated clinical or statistical instrument. If a number is ever used to end a conversation rather than start one, it's being misused.
