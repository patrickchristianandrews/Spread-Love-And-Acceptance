      How it works: tap a square and type; tap it again to switch between across and down. The small numbers match the clues. Some squares belong to only one word, as in many newspaper crosswords. The short, one-word clues are the trickier ones. On a phone, ⊕ makes the squares bigger on the large grids (the grid then scrolls sideways).
