The Kane and Christian Experience. Exploring The Objective Ledger™ framework through narrative, real-world application, and dialogue.

About the podcast: Each episode is a deep dive into one workpaper, chapter, or thematic area. Listen in any order, or follow the episode path matched to the PROG-01 curriculum weeks. Parallel listening while you read or use the tools deepens comprehension and provides voice context for the framework's emotional texture.

Hosted by Kane and Christian with rotating guests, real couple interviews, and listener questions. All episodes are available to paid members of the Survival Kit.
