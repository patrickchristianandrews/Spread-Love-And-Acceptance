About the podcast: Each episode is a deep dive into one workpaper, chapter or theme. Listen in any order, or follow the episode path matched to the PROG-01 curriculum weeks. Listening while you read or use the tools can add a human voice to the ideas.

Hosted by Kane and Christian with rotating guests, real couple interviews, and listener questions. No episodes are published yet. The first three are recorded and coming soon; the rest are in production or planned. When they’re out, the links will appear right here.
