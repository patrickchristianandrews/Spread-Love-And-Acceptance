REPORT-01 · The Full Read

Your progress, week by week

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

One place to type in what your worksheets produced each week, and a report that reads them side by side to show where they agree and where they don’t.

This report brings all Five Pillars together: the load (I), how the setup is holding (II), your load scores (III), how repair is going (IV), and the jobs that quietly slip back (V). In you: you see your own patterns over time. Between you and others: you get one shared picture of the setup, never a verdict on either person.

  1. Your ledger is a file you keep.

    Nothing is stored in the browser or sent anywhere. Press Save ledger to download a small file, and Load ledger next week to carry on.

    Dig deeper: how saving works

  2. Once a week, copy in your numbers.

    After the weekly closing (your short end-of-week review), type the figures from the worksheets you ran: WP-01 minutes, WP-02 scores, WP-03 counts and so on. Leave blank what you didn’t run. It takes about a minute.

    Dig deeper: adding a week

  3. Once a month, add the monthly look-back (WP-04).

    Enter the gaps you found, and how many were back after being fixed last month. That repeat count matters most.

    Dig deeper: adding a month

  4. Read what it writes.

    The report says where things stand, how the numbers fit together, and what to do next. Every note follows a written rule you can check.

    Dig deeper: the report itself

  5. It reads the setup, never a person.

    It never judges either of you or compares you. It is not a clinical tool. If a note is used to end a conversation, it’s being used the wrong way. It’s meant to start one.

    Dig deeper: how the report is written
