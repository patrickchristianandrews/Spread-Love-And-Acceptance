REPORT-01

The Full Read THE COMPILATION LAYER · WHERE EVERYTHING MEETS

One place to record what the worksheets produced, and a report that reads them against each other rather than one at a time.

Each workpaper answers one question well and is blind to the others. WP-01 can't see whether the load is owned. WP-03 can't see whether it's shared. CALC-01 reads three of them and still can't see whether last month's fixes held.

This page is where they're read together. Type in what the worksheets produced — a minute a week — and it writes the part no single instrument can: where the figures agree, where they contradict each other, and which contradiction matters.

Your ledger is a file, not a login. Nothing here is stored in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Press Save ledger to download a small file; press Load ledger next week to pick up where you left off.

That's deliberate. A household's records shouldn't be sitting in the browser of a machine two people share — and a file you keep is one you can put somewhere private, copy, or delete outright.

How This Report Is Written

Nothing here is a model, a prediction, or an opinion. Every finding is a stated rule applied to figures you entered, and each rule is listed below so the output can be checked rather than trusted.

What this report will never contain: a judgment about either person, a comparison between the two of you, or a recommendation about the relationship's future. It reads an arrangement across time. That is its whole scope.

None of it is a validated clinical or statistical instrument. If a finding here is used to end a conversation rather than start one, it is being misused.
