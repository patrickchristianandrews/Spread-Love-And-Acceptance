This worksheet puts Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person to work, with a nod to Pillar V, Notice the quiet incentives. In you: a job that keeps slipping is a setup problem, not a character flaw. Between you and others: every job gets one clear owner, so unclaimed work stops drifting to one person.

Purpose: a living agreement that gives every regular household job exactly one Responsible name and one Accountable name, so you stop re-deciding who owns what every week. (RACI is a planning term from the workplace. It stands for Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed. This treaty uses just the first two.)

  • Responsible (R): does the task.
  • Accountable (A): notices if it didn't get done, and follows up. This can be the same person as R.

Changing the treaty

This treaty isn't set in stone. It's meant to be reworked when life changes (a new job, a health issue, a new baby), not quietly shifted when one person just starts doing more. To change it:

  1. Either of you can ask to revisit it at the weekly closing, your short end-of-week review (Chapter V).
  2. Write each change into the table above. Don't just agree out loud.
  3. You both initial the change.

Signing off

By initialing below, you both confirm that you've read the current version of this table and agree to who owns what, as written. It doesn't mean every task feels perfectly fair. It only means ownership is clear.
