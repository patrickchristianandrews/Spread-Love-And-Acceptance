Say it so it lands

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A quick, kind self-check to run before you answer something that stung, so what you say is something the other person can actually hear.

  1. It is a check on your own next message.

    Nothing is recorded and nobody else’s voice is analysed. Each of you uses it on yourself.

    Dig deeper: what it is for

  2. Before you speak, find three things.

    The fact, in one sentence with no adjectives. The feeling underneath, like tired or unseen. And the ask: what you want to happen next. If your sentence is a judgment on the person, like “you always”, pause and swap it for one fact and one ask.

    Dig deeper: sorting out what you want to say

  3. Before you react, weigh what you heard.

    Is it about a specific task? Would it read the same if you weren’t already stretched? Could a neutral meaning fit? Are you answering their words, or an old argument? If you are stretched or unsure, pause first.

    Dig deeper: weighing what you heard

  4. See it in action.

    “You never think about anyone but yourself” becomes: the bin wasn’t out before pickup, I feel a bit unseen, can we pick a fixed day for it?

    Dig deeper: the worked example

Try this: Next time you feel the urge to snap back, write the fact, the feeling and the ask first, even just in your head.
