WP-02 · The Battery & Stress Meter

How full is your battery?

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A quick check on yourself that separates how much you are already carrying from how upset you are about this one thing.

  1. Use it before a hard conversation.

    Sometimes a small thing feels huge because the rest of the day was heavy. This helps you tell the two apart. It is a structured gut-check, not a medical test.

    Dig deeper: what it measures

  2. Score five things, 0 to 4.

    Each of you does your own, alone, about the last day or two: sleep, workload elsewhere, any unresolved conflict, how your body feels, and time pressure today. 0 means not at all, 4 means very true.

    Dig deeper: the checklist

  3. Add them up and divide by 20.

    Under 0.3: whatever comes up is probably about the thing itself. 0.3 to 0.6: say out loud “heads up, I’m carrying more than usual today.” Over 0.6: put off anything that doesn’t need deciding in the next hour.

    Dig deeper: what each score means

  4. A high score means “later”, not “never”.

    Don’t fill it in about the other person, and don’t use your own score to end a talk you’d rather avoid. It means “let’s revisit this tomorrow.”

    Dig deeper: what it is not for

Try this: Next time something small stings more than it should, score yourself before you answer.
