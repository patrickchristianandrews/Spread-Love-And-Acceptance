Can the load last?

Type in the totals from your worksheets and see whether the way you share the load right now can keep going. It scores the setup, never a person, and never the relationship as a whole. It works for partners, families, roommates, co-carers and small teams: 2 to 8 people. ("Solvency" just means the setup can carry its own weight.)

CALC-01 puts Pillar I, See the whole load and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person to work, with a battery check from Pillar III, Read your state first. In you: you see the whole load you carry as one number. Between you and others: you get one fair read of the setup, never a score on anyone.

What would move the score

    Bands: 0.70 or more, holding. 0.40 up to 0.70, drifting. Below 0.40, time for a kind rethink of the setup. Read from the score rounded to two decimals, so the number and the band always agree. This is a rule-of-thumb worksheet, not a tested model or a compatibility test. It only reflects what you enter, and it can't decide whether a relationship is worth continuing.
