Can the load last?

This calculator uses the three numbers described in Chapter II. It scores how you currently share the workload, not either person, and not the relationship as a whole. ("Solvency" here simply means whether the current setup can keep going.)

CALC-01 puts Pillar I, See the whole load and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person to work. In you: you see the whole load you carry as one number. Between you and others: you get one fair read of the setup, never a score on either person.

From WP-03: the share of tasks that have both a Responsible and an Accountable name.
From WP-02: the average of both people's battery scores this week (how stretched you both are).
