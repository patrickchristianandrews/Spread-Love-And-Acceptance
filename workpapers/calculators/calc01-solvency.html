CALC-01 · The quick read

Is the setup working for everyone?

Also called the solvency read. Works for 2 to 8 people.

Type in the totals from your worksheets and see, in plain words, whether the way you share the load right now is working. It scores the setup, never a person, and never the relationship as a whole. It works for partners, families, roommates, co-carers and small teams: 2 to 8 people. ("Solvency" just means the setup can carry its own weight.)

CALC-01 puts Pillar I, See the whole load and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person to work, with a battery check from Pillar III, Read your state first. In you: you see the whole load you carry as one number. Between you and others: you get one fair read of the setup, never a score on anyone.

Want each input worked out from your raw entries, the apex score and a trend log? Use the full CALC-01. The math is the same on both pages.

Three steps, each counted by how much it matters to the read: balance counts for 40%, ownership for 35% and stress for 25% (the “weights” 0.40, 0.35 and 0.25 you see beside each step).

3. Stress: how much each person is carrying average load · weight 0.25 (25%)

From WP-02, How much are you carrying?: each person's own score, their five 0–4 answers added up and divided by 20. It runs from 0 to 1, and higher means heavier. Everyone fills in their own; nobody guesses for someone else.

What would help most

    The words and their cut-offs: 0.70 or more, working well. 0.40 up to 0.70, needs a look. Below 0.40, needs a rethink, together. Here higher means the setup is working better. Read from the score rounded to two decimals, so the number and the words always agree. This is a rule-of-thumb worksheet, not a tested model or a compatibility test. It only reflects what you enter, and it can't decide whether a relationship is worth continuing. Nothing you type is saved or sent.
