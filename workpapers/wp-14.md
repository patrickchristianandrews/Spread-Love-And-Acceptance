# WP-14 — Is it us, or is it everything else?

*Also called the Static Dampening Log.*

**Purpose:** to separate outside pressure (money, work, illness, broken sleep, family) from friction that really starts between two people, so that outside strain stops being blamed on the other person by default. A one-week log, each person about their own days, read together at the end of the week.

**How often:** one week at a time, whenever life gets heavy or you notice more snapping than usual.

## Why

Stress spillover: in daily-diary studies of newlyweds, spouses under more outside stress reacted more strongly to everyday negative moments in the relationship (Neff & Karney, 2009). After high-workload days, air traffic controllers were more withdrawn with their spouses that evening (Repetti, 1989). Both are correlational.

## Part B — The week at a glance

Each person's heavy days and where the pressure came from; how many tense moments came on a day when someone had a lot of outside pressure; how you sorted them. A pattern to talk about, not proof.

## Made-up example

Mel has a deadline; Dev's mom is in the hospital. 3 of 4 tense moments came on heavy days. "I thought Dev didn't care about the weekend. Really, Dev had been at the hospital all day." Plan: "Rough day, not you" at the door; simple dinners until the deadline passes; Dev's sister covers one hospital visit. The car argument stays "between us", for a calmer day.

## Note

Outside pressure explains a hard day. It doesn't excuse unkindness, and it doesn't make what anyone felt less real. "Between us" moments get their own calm talk (WP-09, Check-ins, or WP-03 if it's about jobs).
