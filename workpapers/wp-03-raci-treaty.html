WP-03 · Domestic RACI Treaty

One owner per job

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A shared list that gives every regular household job one clear owner, so you stop re-deciding who does what every week.

  1. Every job gets two names.

    The Responsible person does the job. The Accountable person notices if it didn’t happen and follows up. It can be the same person.

    Dig deeper: what the two roles mean

  2. Go down the list together.

    Start with the common jobs on the sheet, like groceries, dishes, bills and the family calendar, and add your own rows. For each, write how often it happens and both names.

    Dig deeper: the treaty table

  3. Change it on purpose, not by drift.

    When life changes, either of you can ask for a review at the weekly closing. Write the change into the table and both initial it, rather than one person quietly doing more.

    Dig deeper: how to amend it

  4. Signing means clear, not perfect.

    Initialling says you both agree who owns what as written. It doesn’t say every job feels perfectly fair.

    Dig deeper: ratification

  5. Count how many jobs are fully owned.

    Divide the jobs with both names filled in by all the jobs listed. That is your ownership clarity score, one of the numbers in Chapter II’s check.

    Dig deeper: the ownership clarity score

Try this: Sit down on a calm day and fill in names for the first five jobs. Leave the harder ones for next time.
