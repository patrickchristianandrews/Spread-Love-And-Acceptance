How much are you carrying? A quick stress check

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A kind, quick check on yourself that separates how much you are already carrying from how upset you are about this one thing. The score runs from 0 to 1, and higher means heavier: under 0.30 is a low load, 0.30 up to 0.60 medium, 0.60 and up high.

This worksheet puts Pillar III (“Read your state first”) to work. In you: you check how full your battery is before you judge a moment. Between you and others: you can pick your timing, and say "I'm carrying a lot today" before a hard talk.

  1. Use it before a hard conversation.

    Sometimes a small thing feels huge because the rest of the day was heavy. This helps you tell the two apart. It is a simple gut-check, not a medical test.

    Dig deeper: what it measures

  2. Score five things, 0 to 4.

    Each of you scores only yourself, on your own, thinking about the last day or two: sleep, workload elsewhere, any unresolved conflict, how your body feels, and time pressure today. 0 means not at all, 4 means very true.

    Dig deeper: the checklist

  3. Add them up and divide by 20.

    Under 0.3: whatever comes up is probably about the thing itself. 0.3 to 0.6: say out loud “heads up, I’m carrying more than usual today.” Over 0.6: be gentle with yourself and put off anything that doesn’t need deciding in the next hour.

    Dig deeper: what each score means

  4. A high score means “later,” not “never.”

    Fill it in only about yourself. Use your score to pace a talk, not to dodge one you’d rather avoid. A high score means “let’s come back to this tomorrow.”

    Dig deeper: what it is not for

Try this: Next time something small stings more than it should, score yourself before you answer.
