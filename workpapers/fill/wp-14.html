Is it us, or is it everything else?

A one-week log, a line or two each evening: outside pressure (money, work, health, sleep, family) in one column, tense moments between you in another. At the end of the week, you look at what got blamed on the wrong thing, and make one plan for the pressure.

The ideas behind it: read your state first, and fix the setup, not the person (outside pressure gets a plan, not a culprit). They are two of the site’s Five Pillars, the big ideas everything here is built on.

What you type stays on this device. Nothing you enter in this worksheet is sent anywhere. Nothing is stored unless you turn on “Keep a draft on this device.”
