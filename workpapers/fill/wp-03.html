One owner per job also called RACI Treaty

A living agreement that gives every regular household job exactly one Responsible name and one Accountable name, so you stop re-deciding who owns what every week. Responsible does the task. Accountable notices if it didn't get done and follows up. They can be the same person.

This worksheet puts Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person to work, with a nod to Pillar V, Notice the quiet incentives.
