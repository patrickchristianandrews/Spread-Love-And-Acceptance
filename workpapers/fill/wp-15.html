Keep your little rituals alive

Every three months or so, together: list the small shared habits that keep you close, mark which are alive, fading or stopped, and pick one to restart, small enough to keep on a hard week.

The ideas behind it: tune how you send and receive, over years rather than days, and notice the quiet incentives (the small good things that fade when nobody looks). They are two of the site’s Five Pillars, the big ideas everything here is built on.

What you type stays on this device. Nothing you enter in this worksheet is sent anywhere. Nothing is stored unless you turn on “Keep a draft on this device.”

When you're done, download the PDF and keep it somewhere private. “Save” keeps it in this browser for next time. To keep a copy somewhere else, or to send it, use “Draft file” and open that file on this page later. Or download the fillable PDF: type into it in any PDF app or print it, then open it here again to pick up where you left off.
