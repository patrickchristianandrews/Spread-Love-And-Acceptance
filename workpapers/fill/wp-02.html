How much are you carrying? also called The Battery & Stress Meter

A one-minute check that each person fills in about themselves. It separates "How much am I already carrying?" from "How upset am I about this one thing?" Higher numbers mean more load. It is not a clinical test, just a structured gut-check.

This worksheet puts Pillar III, Read your state first to work.

What you type stays on this device. Nothing you enter in this worksheet is sent anywhere. Nothing is stored unless you turn on “Keep a draft on this device”.

When you're done, download the PDF and keep it somewhere private. To finish later, save a draft file and open it on this page next time. Or download the fillable PDF: type into it in any PDF app or print it, then open it here again to pick up where you left off.
