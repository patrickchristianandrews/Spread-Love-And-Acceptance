# WP-04 — Unbilled Deficit Audit

**Purpose:** a monthly reconciliation, done together, that looks across four weeks of Field Audit logs (WP-01) and the RACI Treaty (WP-03) to find tasks that keep becoming deficits — not to tally what either person "owes," but to find where the household's structure needs a fix rather than another one-off patch.

**Cadence:** monthly. Not weekly — this audit is specifically for patterns that are invisible at the weekly scale.

**Structural gap** — no clear owner exists, or the named owner isn't the person actually handling it in practice.
*Fix:* update the RACI Treaty (WP-03) this session.

**Capacity issue** — has a named owner, but that person has been consistently unable to keep up with it.
*Fix:* a direct conversation using a neutral refusal or reassignment (WP-01, WP-09) — not a private resolution to just try harder.

**One-off, no action** — flagged more than once but tied to an identifiable temporary cause (illness, travel, a busy work month).
*Fix:* note it and move on; don't let a temporary cause justify a permanent structural change.

## What this audit is not for

- **Not a retroactive bill.** Hours already passed don't get tallied up and "charged" to anyone. The audit only looks backward to find the pattern, and only acts forward on the structure.
- **Not a performance review.** A task with no owner is a gap in the treaty, not a verdict on the person who happened to keep covering it.
- **Not a substitute for the weekly closing.** This audit works because the weekly rhythm (Lemonade Stand, RACI glance) keeps feeding it real data. Skipping the weekly checks makes this audit far less accurate.
