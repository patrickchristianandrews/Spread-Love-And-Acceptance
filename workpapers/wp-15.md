# WP-15 — Keep your little rituals alive

*Also called Harmonic Resonance & Entrainment.*

**Purpose:** long-cycle calibration: the shared touchpoints that keep two people in step across years rather than weeks, and what to do when they've quietly lapsed.

**How often:** about every three months. The change of season is an easy reminder. About 15 minutes, together.

Pages: [simple](wp-15-keep-your-little-rituals-alive.html) · [in depth](wp-15-keep-your-little-rituals-alive-in-depth.html) · [fill in](fill/wp-15.html)

## Why

A review of 50 years of research linked family routines and rituals with marital satisfaction, parenting confidence and children's wellbeing, while noting weak measurement and convenience samples (Fiese et al., 2002). Couples and friends share many small everyday rituals (Bruess & Pearson, 1997). Couples who did a new, mildly challenging activity together reported higher relationship quality afterward in short lab experiments (Aron et al., 2000). The name: entrainment, two linked rhythms falling into step (Huygens' pendulum clocks, 1665).

## Made-up example

June and Ray: coffee before work (daily, alive); Friday takeout and a film (weekly, fading); Sunday walk (weekly, stopped when Saturday shifts started); birthday breakfast in bed (yearly, alive); one new place a month (new idea). Restart: the walk, twenty minutes round the park after lunch; June suggests it this Sunday. Let go, with thanks: Thursday quiz night.

## Note

A ritual is something you would both miss, not a test of love. A missed one is just a missed one.
