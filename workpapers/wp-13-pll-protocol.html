WP-13 · Phase-Locked Loop

The 90-second daily check-in

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A 90-second daily check-in that makes small corrections every day, so little things don’t pile up into a big weekend argument.

This check-in puts Pillar IV, Tune how you send and receive to work, with a nod to Pillar V, Notice the quiet incentives. In you: you take a quick reading of your own day. Between you and others: small daily corrections keep you in tune, and a daily thank-you keeps things steady.

  1. Small and often beats big and rare.

    A phase-locked loop is a circuit that keeps two signals in step. This check-in does the same for two people, with tiny daily nudges instead of one big jolt.

    Dig deeper: why it works

  2. Once a day, one sentence each.

    Pick a regular moment, like after dinner. Each of you says how full you were today (low, medium or high), one thing you appreciated about the other, one small thing that didn’t feel great, and one thing that would help tomorrow. The last two are optional.

    Dig deeper: the daily loop

  3. Just listen. No need to debate.

    One speaks, the other takes it in. Nothing gets solved here. If it needs a real talk, save it for the weekly closing (your short end-of-week review).

    Dig deeper: keeping it short

  4. Once a week, look for repeats.

    Skim the week’s notes. Anything that came up more than twice moves to your job list (WP-03) or your tone check (WP-09) instead of just hanging around.

    Dig deeper: the weekly catch-up

  5. Keep it a quick update, not a complaint.

    If the check-in turns into criticism, it stops feeling safe and you’ll both start to skip it. Keep the daily one to plain, kind facts.

    Dig deeper: what to watch for

Try this: Choose your moment tonight and try it. Start with the appreciation, because it sets the tone for the rest. The whole thing takes about 90 seconds.
