The 90-second daily check-in

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A 90-second daily check-in that makes small corrections every day, so little things don’t build up into a big weekend argument.

  1. Small and often beats big and rare.

    Like the circuit it is named after, it keeps two people in step with tiny daily nudges instead of one big yank.

    Dig deeper: why it works

  2. Once a day, one sentence each.

    Pick a regular moment, like after dinner. Each of you says how full you were today (low, medium or high), one thing you appreciated about the other, one small thing that didn’t feel great, and one thing that would help tomorrow. The last two are optional.

    Dig deeper: the daily loop

  3. Just listen. No debating.

    One speaks, the other takes it in. Nothing gets solved here. If it needs a real talk, save it for the weekly closing.

    Dig deeper: keeping it short

  4. Once a week, look for repeats.

    Skim the week’s notes. Anything that came up more than twice moves to your job list (WP-03) or your tone check (WP-09) instead of just hanging around.

    Dig deeper: the weekly resync

  5. Keep it a status, not a complaint.

    If the check-in turns into criticism, you’ll both start avoiding it. Keep the daily one to plain facts.

    Dig deeper: what to watch for

Try this: Choose your moment tonight and try it. Start with the appreciation: it sets the tone for the rest, and it takes about 90 seconds.
