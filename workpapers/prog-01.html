PROG-01: The 6-Week Guided Implementation Roadmap

A structured, sequential path through The Objective Ledger™ framework. Each week integrates chapters, workpapers, podcast episodes, and audio entrainment into a cohesive progression.

How to use this curriculum: Follow the weeks in order. Each week is designed to take 5–7 hours of engagement (reading, working through tools, listening, practicing). You don't need to wait until week one is perfect to start week two. This is not a sprint—it's a structured on-ramp. At the end of week six, you'll have completed the foundational framework and be ready for ongoing application.

Partner participation is optional but recommended. Some weeks ask you both to read chapters or use tools together. If your partner isn't ready yet, you can still complete the curriculum individually.

Week 1

Foundation & Self-Audit

Establish baseline state. Build vocabulary. Begin logging.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Preface
Unbilled Debt
Read the opening framing. This is short and essential context for why you're building a shared ledger. Understand the problem statement before you engage with solutions.
Chapter I
The Radio Frequency Paradigm
Learn the metaphor. This is the conceptual core—two signals slightly out of sync create interference. Not a diagnosis. A shared vocabulary for getting back in rhythm.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

Check Your State
Nervous System Ladder (Polyvagal Self-Check)
Use the three-rung ladder. Understand your current nervous system state before every significant conversation. This becomes your baseline for the week.
WP-01
Daily Balance Sheet & Neutral Refusals
Start logging daily tasks and emotional labor for 7 days. You're not sharing this yet—just building awareness of invisible work. Familiarize yourself with the three Neutral Refusals scripts. Use them if a request comes in that you need to decline or defer.

📻 Listen (1 hour)

📻 Podcast Episode 1: "Unbilled Debt: Why Your Ledger Has Only One Column"
Lamonz explores the core problem: invisible labor accumulates when only one person sees the books. Parallel listening while you read deepens the framework's emotional resonance. Listen at 1x speed to catch nuance. (~28 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Baseline Reset" — 40Hz Binaural Stems (5 min)
Use this at the end of week 1 to establish your baseline nervous system state. Simple, grounding. Prepares you for week 2's deeper work.
Expected Output by End of Week 1
You've read the Preface and Chapter I. You've logged 7 days of work in WP-01. You know the three Neutral Refusals by memory. You've listened to the podcast episode once (or twice). You have a baseline nervous system awareness. You're ready to name ownership.
Week 2

Ownership Clarity & Domestic Governance

Name who owns what. Eliminate ambiguous shared responsibilities.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter II
The Epistemic Verdict Engine & P(Solvency)
Understand the sustainability formula. How do you know if the current workload arrangement is actually working? This chapter gives you the heuristic and explains each component.
Chapter III
The Lemonade Stand Heuristic
Why shared visibility matters. One person makes the lemonade, one sells it. Both are necessary. If only one keeps the books, the other can never know if they're fair. Read this together with your partner if possible.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

WP-03
Household Chore Treaty (Domestic RACI Governance)
Every household domain gets one owner. Responsible. Accountable. No more shared ambiguity. Go through your household systems (cooking, laundry, finances, childcare, appointments, etc.) and assign single-point ownership. This is the most practically impactful tool of week 2.
CALC-01
Solvency Read (P(Solvency) Calculator)
Feed your week 1 WP-01 data and this week's emerging ownership clarity into CALC-01. Generate your first P(Solvency) score. Is it above 0.70? Between 0.40 and 0.70? Below 0.40? This tells you where intervention is needed.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 2: "The Radio Frequency Paradigm in Action"
Lamonz breaks down pace, register, and urgency mismatch with real examples. Shows how two reasonable people can create interference without intending to. (~31 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 3: "The Lemonade Stand: Why One Person's Books Aren't Enough"
Direct deep-dive into the foundational heuristic. Why shared accounting changes everything. (~26 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Ownership Clarity" — 40Hz + Sub-Bass Composite (10 min)
Use before having the "who owns what" conversation with your partner. Grounding + activation. Gets you calm but present.
Expected Output by End of Week 2
You've read Chapters II and III (ideally together with your partner). You've completed WP-03 and named ownership for every household domain. You have a P(Solvency) score. You can explain the Lemonade Stand to someone else. You're ready to look at the numbers.
Week 3

Numerics & Health Tracking

Quantify workload. Monitor biological safety. Detect early extraction.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter IV
Deontological Parity & Sensory Gating
How do two people assess fairness when neither has perfect information about the other's internal state? This chapter frames the epistemology of balance under uncertainty. Dense but essential.

🔧 Tools (2 hours)

WP-02
Battery & Stress Meter (Autonomic Saturation Index)
Track your physiological state daily for 7 days on a 0–4 scale. Is your nervous system in the green? Yellow? Red? This becomes essential early-warning data. If one partner is consistently at 3–4, the arrangement isn't sustainable.
CALC-02
30-Day Ledger Spread (Rolling Balance Sheet)
Combine weeks 1–3 of WP-01 data into a 30-day net spread. Who executed what tasks? How many emergency rescues happened? What's the cumulative imbalance?

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 4: "Your Nervous System is Data"
Lamonz and guests discuss polyvagal theory, why your body knows when the arrangement is unsustainable before your mind does, and how to trust somatic signals. (~33 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 5: "The Extraction Cycle in Slow Motion"
How invisible work compounds into resentment. Real household examples from listeners. What extraction looks like at each stage. (~28 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Saturation Release" — 40Hz + Sub-Bass (15 min)
Use when your Battery meter hits 3 or higher. Down-regulates autonomic arousal. Listen before bed or during a break.
Expected Output by End of Week 3
You've read Chapter IV. You have 3 weeks of WP-01 data logged and a 30-day spread. You have 7 days of Battery tracking. You can see patterns: Who's carrying more? When does saturation spike? Where are the extraction cycles hiding? You're ready to talk about communication.
Week 4

Communication & Tone

Talk about the ledger without triggering defensiveness. Strip tone from content.

📖 Read (1 hour)

Chapter V (Preview)
Communication Protocols (Advanced Chapter)
How to open the ledger conversation without starting a fight. This chapter is longer and more advanced, but week 4 gives you a preview of its core ideas: naming the frame, managing tone, validating before arguing.

🔧 Tools (3 hours)

WP-09
Tone Transducer & Emotional Translation Filter
Learn to strip aggressive inflection from communication. When your partner says "You never help," the tone carrier wave says "I'm exhausted and invisible." The filter separates content from delivery. Practice translating reactive tone into objective data. Do this with a partner if possible—it's a dialogue tool.
WP-10 (Preview)
Ocular Convergence Spider Compass (7-Axis Radar)
Plot your current state across seven vectors: workload, autonomy, communication, frequency alignment, nervous system state, ownership clarity, and relational trust. Which vector is lowest? That's where intervention should start.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 6: "Opening the Ledger: How to Talk About the Numbers"
Lamonz interviews couples who've successfully opened their shared ledger. What worked? What failed? What did they wish they'd known? (~35 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 7: "Tone Matters: Separating Content from Delivery"
A deep linguistic dive into WP-09. Why tone is data. How to hear it without defensiveness. How to notice your own inflection. (~29 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Conversation Readiness" — 40Hz Binaural + Composite (20 min)
Use before difficult conversations with your partner. Ventral vagal activation—calm + social. Listen 20 minutes before you sit down to talk numbers.
Expected Output by End of Week 4
You've read Chapter V preview. You can run WP-09 (Tone Transducer) on a real conversation. You have a 7-axis Spider Compass plot showing your current system health. You understand which vector needs the most support. You're ready to talk to your partner about the ledger data using non-defensive language.
Week 5

Frequency Alignment & Synchronization

Diagnose heterodyne feedback. Achieve phase-locked loop resonance.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter VI (Preview)
The Radio Frequency Deep Dive: Processing Speed & Rhythm
Return to the frequency metaphor at greater depth. Pace, register, urgency. How do two people with different processing speeds maintain resonance? This chapter explores the engineering beneath the poetry.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

WP-12
Carrier Frequency Audit & Misalignment Matrix
Map your individual processing speeds, communication cadences, and cognitive pacing. Where do you and your partner mismatch? Does one of you mobilize quickly while the other needs time? Do you have different urgency windows? Diagnosis comes first.
WP-13
Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) Synchronization Protocol
Once you've diagnosed the mismatch, use PLL to lock back into resonance. This is a step-by-step communication protocol: structured turn-taking, mutual validation loops, shared rhythm. Practice this weekly, especially when discussing ledger data or relationship concerns.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 8: "Processing Speed: Why You Mobilize Faster Than They Do"
Lamonz explores pace differences, activation timelines, and why one person's "when you get a chance" is another person's immediate emergency. (~32 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 9: "The Phase-Locked Loop: Getting In Sync When You're Out of Sync"
A practical walkthrough of WP-13. How to use structured turn-taking and validation loops to lock back into resonance. Includes real dialogue examples. (~26 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Harmonic Alignment" — Composite 40Hz + Entrainment Stem (20 min)
Use before and after practicing WP-13 with your partner. Encourages neurological synchronization and mutual regulation.
Expected Output by End of Week 5
You've read Chapter VI preview. You've completed WP-12 (Carrier Frequency Audit) and identified your mismatches. You've practiced WP-13 (PLL Protocol) at least once with your partner. You can feel the difference between heterodyne feedback (chaos) and phase-locked resonance (calm). You're ready for long-term stability work.
Week 6

Long-Term Stability & Entrainment

Build sustainable practices. Maintain resonance over months and years.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter VII (Preview)
Long-Term Relational Equilibrium & Seasonal Rhythms
Six weeks in, you've built the foundation. But the framework isn't a sprint to completion—it's a living system. How do you maintain resonance as circumstances change? As kids grow, careers shift, health fluctuates? This chapter looks at multi-year stability.

🔧 Tools (2 hours)

WP-14
White Noise & Static Dampening Log
Not all relational stress is relational. Sleep deprivation, financial pressure, professional burnout—these are environmental statics, not structural relational debt. Use WP-14 to separate the two. This prevents blame cycles rooted in external factors.
WP-15
Harmonic Resonance & Long-Term Entrainment Workpaper
Design your weekly calibration ritual. What shared somatic, intellectual, and emotional touchpoints will keep you locked in resonance? A weekly check-in? A walk? A joint review of the ledger? Make it sustainable, not another chore.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 10: "External Static: When the Stress Isn't Relational"
Lamonz interviews therapists and couples about separating structural relational issues from external life pressure. How to support your partner through burnout without confusing it with abandonment. (~30 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 11: "Building a Ritual: How to Stay in Resonance for the Long Term"
Closing episode. Listeners share their calibration rituals—some silly, some serious, all effective. How to make the framework part of your actual life, not a separate project. (~24 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Long-Term Resonance" — Full Reset Stack Composite (20 min)
The complete binaural + sub-bass + entrainment stem. Use weekly as part of your calibration ritual. This is your maintenance audio—not a crisis tool, but a sustenance tool.
Expected Output by End of Week 6
You've read the seven-chapter preview of the full manuscript. You've completed all four system layers of workpapers (WP-01 through WP-15, at least the core ones). You have a designed weekly calibration ritual. You understand the framework as a living practice, not a one-time audit. You're ready to maintain this over months and years. Congratulations—the 6-week curriculum is complete.

What happens after week 6? You have two paths:

Path 1: Ongoing application. Keep using WP-01 and WP-02 weekly. Run your calibration ritual. Refresh your P(Solvency) score monthly. The framework becomes part of how you communicate and stay in balance.

Path 2: Deep dives. Return to chapters V–XII (the full manuscript) and explore specific topics: crisis protocols, multi-partner systems, advanced frequency analysis, seasonal rhythm management. The full program gives you the theory and tools to customize the framework for your specific situation.
