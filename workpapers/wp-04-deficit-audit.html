WP-04 · Unbilled Deficit Audit

What keeps coming back?

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

Once a month, together, you take a gentle look back at four weeks of logs to find the jobs that keep slipping, and fix the setup instead of patching it again.

  1. Do it monthly, not weekly.

    Some patterns only show up across a month. You need your four weekly WP-01 logs and your WP-03 job list to hand.

    Dig deeper: what the look-back is for

  2. List the jobs that slipped.

    Write down every job that was noticed but not done, or only done after being raised more than once, in any week. Tick each week it happened. Three or four ticks is a real pattern, not a fluke.

    Dig deeper: gathering the month’s notes

  3. Sort each repeat into one of three kinds.

    No clear owner, or the named owner isn’t the one doing it: update WP-03. The owner can’t keep up: have a kind, honest conversation. A temporary cause like illness or travel: note it and move on.

    Dig deeper: sorting each gap

  4. Wrap it up and keep count.

    Make the changes, book any conversation you need, and write down how many ownership gaps you found. Fewer each month means it is working. More just means something bigger changed, and it’s worth a talk.

    Dig deeper: wrapping up the look-back

  5. Nobody owes anything.

    Past hours don’t get charged to anyone. A job with no owner is a gap in the list, not a judgment on whoever kept covering it.

    Dig deeper: what the look-back is not for

Try this: Put a date in the calendar for the end of this month, and keep your weekly logs until then.
