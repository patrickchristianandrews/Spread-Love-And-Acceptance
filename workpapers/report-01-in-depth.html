The Full Read WHERE ALL YOUR WORKSHEETS COME TOGETHER

One place to record what your worksheets produced, and a report that reads them side by side instead of one at a time.

This report brings all Five Pillars together: the load (I), how the setup is holding (II), your load scores (III), how repair is going (IV), and the jobs that quietly slip back (V). In you: you see your own patterns over time. Between you and others: you get one shared picture of the setup, never a verdict on either person.

Where the numbers come from: the fill-in workpapers · CALC-01 · Build your Workpaper Suite

Each worksheet answers one question well, but it can't see the others. WP-01 can't see whether each job has an owner. WP-03 can't see whether the work is shared fairly. CALC-01 reads three of them and still can't see whether last month's fixes held.

This page is where they're read together. Type in what your worksheets produced. It takes about a minute a week. The page then writes what no single worksheet can: where the numbers agree, where they disagree, and which disagreement matters.

Your ledger is a file, not a login. Nothing here is stored in this browser, and nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere. Press Save ledger to download a small file. Next week, press Load ledger to pick up where you left off.

That's on purpose. A household's records shouldn't sit in the browser of a computer two people share. A file you keep is one you can put somewhere private, copy, or delete completely.

How This Report Is Written

Nothing here is a model, a prediction or an opinion. Every finding comes from a written rule applied to the numbers you entered. Each rule is listed below, so you can check the report instead of just trusting it.

What this report will never contain: a judgment about either person, a comparison between the two of you, or advice about the relationship's future. It reads how your setup changes over time. That is all it does.

None of it is a tested clinical or statistical tool. If a finding here is used to end a conversation instead of starting one, it is being misused.
