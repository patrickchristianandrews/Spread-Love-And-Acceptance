Pal Cam TV: a cozy cartoon puppy cam

Tidbit and Sugarfoot on the big screen, all day. Press start and leave it running. The scene changes every so often, and the light follows the real time of day, so evenings get cozy and nights get dark.

Can’t find your TV? Other live ways to watch

Every way below shows the real, live Pal Cam, not a recording.

  • Chrome on a computer: open this page, then the ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Cast…, choose Sources → Cast tab, pick your TV, and press Start Pal Cam TV.
  • Android phone: swipe down twice, tap Screen Cast (or Smart View), pick your TV, then press Start Pal Cam TV here.
  • iPhone or iPad with an Apple TV or AirPlay TV: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, pick your TV, then press Start Pal Cam TV.
  • A TV with a web browser, or a computer plugged into the TV: open this page on it and press Start Pal Cam TV.
  • The Cast button above needs the TV and your phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi. If the TV still doesn’t show up, restart the Chromecast and try again in a few minutes.
  • Sound starts on: soft barks, little chimes, playful game sounds (coins, cards, dice and a small bell), and quiet sounds of the place, like birds or waves. Gentle music plays too; press M or tap Music to turn it off.
  • On a phone or tablet, buttons for Sound, Captions, Music and Exit stay at the top of the screen.
  • With a keyboard, press Esc (or Back on a TV remote) to come back to this screen.
  • Press C to show or hide the captions, S to turn the pups’ soft sounds on or off, and M for gentle background music.
  • For all-day use, plug the device in and turn off its sleep and screen saver too.
