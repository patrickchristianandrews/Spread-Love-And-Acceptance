Watch it on your TV

  • Chromecast or Google TV: use Chrome on a computer or an Android phone, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, and press Cast. The TV joins the station at the moment that’s playing now and keeps going on its own, so you can close this page.
  • Apple TV or AirPlay: on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, tune in, then press Play on a TV (or the AirPlay button in full screen) and pick your TV.
  • A smart TV with a web browser: open this page on the TV itself and press Tune in, then Full screen.
