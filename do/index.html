Try the workpapers

See how the worksheets work on a worked example before you use your own week.

The Playground lets you try Pillar I, See the whole load, Pillar III, Read your state first and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person in a few minutes. In you: you notice what you really carry. Between you and others: you see how clear owners change the picture.

The Playground uses an example household of three roommates, with real math you can change. It works the same way for partners, families, co-carers and teams at work: the real worksheets take 2 to 8 people.

What you can try

  • WP-01, Who did what: log a week and see how the hours split (Pillar I)
  • WP-02, How much are you carrying?: five quick questions about how much you're carrying (higher = heavier) (Pillar III)
  • WP-03, One owner per job: give every regular job a named owner (Pillar II)
  • CALC-01, Is the setup working for both of you?: put the three together and see what would move the score
