Learn by doing: practice the worksheets on an example

Also called the Workpaper Playground

See how the worksheets work on a worked example before you use your own week.

The Playground lets you try Pillar I, See the whole load, Pillar III, Read your state first and Pillar II, Fix the setup, not the person in a few minutes. In you: you notice what you really carry. Between you and others: you see how clear owners change the picture.

The Playground uses an example household of three roommates, with real math you can change. It works the same way for partners, families, co-caregivers and teams at work: the real worksheets take 2 to 8 people.

Enter the Playground

What you can try

  • WP-01, Who did what: log a week and see how the hours split (Pillar I)
  • WP-02, How much are you carrying? Five quick questions about your own day. Higher means a fuller stress tank (Pillar III)
  • WP-03, One owner per job: give every regular job a named owner (Pillar II)
  • CALC-01, Is the setup working for everyone? Put the three together and see what would move the score

Nothing is saved, and no email is needed. When you're ready, the fill-in workpapers are the real thing, and the Workpaper Suite builds a full set for your situation.
