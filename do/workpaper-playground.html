WP-01: Field Audit — Map Your Workload

See who's actually doing what in your household right now. The Field Audit is a simple list: every task, who does it, how often. No judgment. Just clarity.

What you'll do: Spend 10 minutes listing the things that actually take time in your household. Estimate hours per week. The tool shows you the distribution.

This is the first step. You can't fix what you can't see.

WP-03: Telemetry Metrics — Track Over Time

A weekly check-in: workload hours, satisfaction, nervous system state, clarity on ownership. Not for perfectionists. For people who want to notice patterns before they become problems.

What you'll do: Spend 5 minutes once a week logging the metrics that matter. The tool tracks them over 12 weeks so you can see what's actually trending.

CALC-01: Solvency Read — Is This Sustainable?

Feed in your workload data from the Field Audit, plus clarity scores and nervous system load. The calculator returns a single number: P(Solvency), your probability that the current arrangement can carry its own weight.

What you'll get: A number between 0.0 and 1.0. Above 0.70 = sustainable. Below 0.40 = something needs to change. Between = something is drifting.
