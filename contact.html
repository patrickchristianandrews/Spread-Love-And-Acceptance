Contact us

Email spreadloveandacceptance@gmail.com. Tapping it opens your email app. If nothing opens, copy the address and write from any email account. This site can’t send email by itself, so there is no form here.

What helps in a message

  • What helped, what was confusing, what is missing, or an idea.
  • Which page or tool it was about.
  • If something isn’t working, what you were doing and which device and browser you use.
  • Please leave out private details about other people.

This site is made by one person, so a reply can take a little while. Every message is read.
