What is never kept

What you type into the chat, the tools and the worksheets is never sent anywhere. (Your chat with Professor Puddles is kept only in this tab, listed above, until you close it or press Start over.) If you turn on the weather, a rounded spot from your location goes to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to look up the forecast. Google Analytics, which counts visits, keeps its own cookies (their names start with “_ga”); this page can’t see or erase them, but your browser’s settings can, and blocking them doesn’t change anything on the site. Your email address is sent to the newsletter service only when you choose to sign up, and the site keeps a copy in this browser so every page stays open for you. How we handle your information
