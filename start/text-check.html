What it looks for

  1. Words that can sting: “always” and “never”, name-calling, sarcasm and digs.
  2. Blame and guesses about why they did it, and old arguments brought back in.
  3. Your ask: whether it’s clear what you want, and by when. The clearer version keeps it.

What it can’t know: it reads the words only. It can’t hear your tone, see a face or know your history together, and the same words can be warm between two people and cutting between two others. Treat it as a second opinion, not a verdict.
