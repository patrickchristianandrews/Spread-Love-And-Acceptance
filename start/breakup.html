When you want to text them, start here.

Wanting to message an ex is very common, especially late at night. It usually means you miss them. It doesn’t mean you have to send it.

Tonight, and the first days

  1. Write it, don’t send it. Type it in your notes, not in your chat with them. Read it again in the morning.
  2. Text a friend instead: “I really want to message my ex right now. Can you talk me out of it for ten minutes?” Then mute or archive the chat with your ex.
  3. Keep three basics going: some sleep, a proper meal, and daylight every day.
  4. Tell one or two people what would help: “We’ve split up. I don’t need advice yet. Could you text me in the evenings this week?”
  5. Hold off on big decisions you don’t have to make this week. Today you only need to get through today.
