Companion Album

Echoes of Gold

(The Christian Project) — The Prelude to Analytical R&B

Christian, performing as Loves Journey EP · Released February 6, 2026 · 11 songs, 41 minutes

Every system starts somewhere unrecorded. Before The Objective Ledger had a website, a workpaper, or a single formula, it had a soundtrack. This album was produced entirely by Christian, the same person who later built spreadloveandacceptance.com, and it came first. The math came after. The feeling came before the framework that tries to hold it.

That order is the whole point of this project. The practical and the felt aren't two separate things running side by side, kept apart so neither embarrasses the other. They're meant to meet. The workpapers make the practical side easy to read: hours logged, ownership assigned, a number you can point to. This album carries what the numbers were never going to reach. The project only works when both sides reach toward the same point at once. This project calls that point, half seriously, the third eye: the moment the practical and the felt stop competing for attention and simply line up.
