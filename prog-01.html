The 6-Week Guided Implementation Roadmap

Simple versionRead the full program

A six-week path through the program, one step at a time. Follow the weeks in order. It works best with the other person, but you can do it on your own.

  1. Week 1: See the work that’s already happening.

    Read the Preface and Chapter I. Check how you are doing before hard talks, and log a week of who does what in WP-01, a daily balance sheet. You are not sharing it yet.

    Dig deeper: week 1, foundation

  2. Week 2: Give every job one owner.

    Read Chapter II. Use WP-03 to name one owner for each regular job, so nothing is left vaguely shared. Then get a first read from CALC-01 on whether the split looks sustainable.

    Dig deeper: week 2, ownership

  3. Week 3: Notice how loaded each of you is.

    Read Chapter IV. Use WP-02, a short stress check, each day for a week. Look for patterns: who is carrying more, and when stress spikes.

    Dig deeper: week 3, the numbers

  4. Week 4: Talk about it without a fight.

    Learn to hear what someone means apart from how it came out. WP-09 helps you turn a raw reaction into fact, feeling and a clear ask.

    Dig deeper: week 4, communication

  5. Week 5: Get back in step.

    Notice where your pace differs: one of you may move fast while the other needs time. Practise the WP-13 check-in together at least once.

    Dig deeper: week 5, getting in sync

  6. Week 6: Make it last.

    Separate outside stress, like poor sleep or money worries, from how you share the work. Then choose a simple weekly ritual that keeps you in step without becoming another chore.

    Dig deeper: week 6, long-term habits

Try this: Start week 1: each of you logs one week of who does what.
