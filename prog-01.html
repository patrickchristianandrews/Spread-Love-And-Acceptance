PROG-01 · The 6-Week Guided Implementation Roadmap

Six gentle weeks

Simple version 🌱 Still growingRead the full program

A six-week path through the program, one step at a time. Follow the weeks in order. It works best with the other person, but you can do it on your own.

  1. Week 1: See the work that’s already happening.

    Read the Preface and Chapter I. Before any hard talks, check how you are doing with Today’s Weather. Then jot down a week of who does what in the Field Audit (WP-01, “Who did what”), a simple daily log. You are not sharing it yet.

    Dig deeper: week 1, foundation

  2. Week 2: Give every job one owner.

    Read Chapter II. Use the RACI Treaty (WP-03, “One owner per job”) to name one owner for each regular job, so nothing is left vaguely shared. Then get a first read on whether the split can last from the Solvency Read (CALC-01).

    Dig deeper: week 2, ownership

  3. Week 3: Notice how full each of your batteries is.

    Read Chapters III and IV. Use the Battery & Stress Meter (WP-02), a short stress check, each day for a week. Look for patterns: who is carrying more, and when stress runs high.

    Dig deeper: week 3, the numbers

  4. Week 4: Talk about it kindly.

    Learn to hear what someone means apart from how it came out. The Tone Filter (WP-09, “Say it so it lands”) helps you turn a raw reaction into fact, feeling and a clear ask. The Calm-Down Kit (WP-11) is where you agree on your pause line on a calm day.

    Dig deeper: week 4, communication

  5. Week 5: Get back in step.

    Notice where your pace differs: one of you may move fast while the other needs time. Try the 90-second daily check-in (WP-13) together at least once.

    Dig deeper: week 5, getting in sync

  6. Week 6: Make it last.

    Run the Field Audit (WP-01) again and compare it with week 1. Separate outside stress, like poor sleep or money worries, from how you share the work. Then choose a simple weekly ritual that keeps you in step without becoming another chore. Turning Toward has ideas.

    Dig deeper: week 6, long-term habits
