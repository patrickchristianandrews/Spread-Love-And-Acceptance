PROG-01: The 6-Week Guided Implementation Roadmap

A structured, sequential path through The Objective Ledger framework. Each week brings together chapters, workpapers, podcast episodes and optional audio in one steady progression.

Full version← Back to the simple version

How to use this curriculum: Follow the weeks in order. Each week takes about 5 to 7 hours in total (reading, using the tools, listening and practicing). You don't need to wait until week one is perfect to start week two. This is not a sprint. It's a gentle, structured way in. By the end of week six, you'll have worked through the core of the framework and be ready to keep using it.

The other person's participation is optional but recommended. The program works for any two people who share time, space, work or care: partners, family members, roommates, co-parents, coworkers or caregivers (see how it fits your relationships). Some weeks ask you both to read chapters or use tools together. If the other person isn't ready yet, you can still complete the curriculum individually.

Week 1

Foundation & Self-Audit

Learn your starting point. Learn the key words. Start logging.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Preface
Unbilled Debt
Read the opening. It's short, and it explains why you're building a shared ledger. Understand the problem before you look at solutions.
Chapter I
Why we get out of tune
Learn the metaphor. This is the central idea: two signals slightly out of sync create interference. Not a diagnosis. A shared vocabulary for getting back in rhythm.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

Check Your State
Today’s Weather (Nervous-System Self-Check)
Use the sky check: clear, gusty or fogged in. Know your own state before every significant conversation. Log it each day, and it becomes your baseline for the week.
WP-01
Who did what, and kind ways to say no
Start logging daily tasks and emotional labor for 7 days. You're not sharing this yet—just building awareness of invisible work. Get to know the three scripts for saying no kindly. Use them if a request comes in that you need to decline or defer.

📻 Listen (1 hour)

📻 Podcast Episode 1: "Unbilled Debt: Why Your Ledger Has Only One Column"
Kane and Christian explore the core problem: invisible labor accumulates when only one person sees the books. Listening alongside your reading adds a human voice to the ideas. (~28 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Shooting Star" — soft, floating instrumental (3:27)
Optional. A few quiet minutes at the end of the week, before week 2. It’s in Brain Breakers; no workpaper requires audio.
Expected Output by End of Week 1
You've read the Preface and Chapter I. You've logged 7 days of work in WP-01. You know the three kind ways to say no by heart. You've listened to the podcast episode once (or twice). You have a week of Today’s Weather readings. You're ready to name who owns what.
Week 2

Ownership Clarity: One Owner per Job

Name who owns what. End vague, shared responsibilities.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter II
Is the split working?
Understand the sustainability formula. How do you know if the current workload arrangement is actually working? This chapter gives you the rule of thumb and explains each part.
Free tool
The Lemonade Stand
Why shared visibility matters. One person makes the lemonade, one sells it. Both are necessary. If only one keeps the books, the other can never know if they're fair. Try it together with the other person if possible.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

WP-03
One Owner per Job
Every area of the household gets one owner, who is both responsible and accountable. No more vague sharing. Go through your household systems (cooking, laundry, finances, childcare, appointments and so on) and give each one a single owner. This is the most useful tool of week 2.
CALC-01
Is the setup working for everyone? (CALC-01)
Enter your week 1 WP-01 numbers and this week's ownership results into CALC-01. Generate your first setup score. Is it above 0.70? Between 0.40 and 0.70? Below 0.40? This tells you where change is needed.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 2: "The Radio Frequency Paradigm in Action"
Kane and Christian break down mismatches in pace, register (tone) and urgency, with real examples. It shows how two reasonable people can create interference without intending to. (~31 min)
📻 Podcast Episode 3: "The Lemonade Stand: Why One Person's Books Aren't Enough"
A deep dive into the Lemonade Stand, the program's basic rule of thumb. Why shared accounting changes everything. (~26 min)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Shooting Star" — soft, floating instrumental (3:27)
Optional. Some people like a quiet few minutes before the "who owns what" conversation. It’s in Brain Breakers.
Expected Output by End of Week 2
You've read Chapter II and tried the Lemonade Stand (ideally together with the other person). You've completed WP-03 and named an owner for every area of the household. You have a setup score. You can explain the Lemonade Stand to someone else. You're ready to look at the numbers.
Week 3

The Numbers & Your Batteries

Measure the workload. Notice load early. Spot the extraction cycle (how unclaimed work turns into resentment) before it grows.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Chapter IV
Two kinds of fair
How do two people assess fairness when neither has perfect information about the other's internal state? This chapter looks at how to judge balance when you can't see everything. It's dense, but essential.
Chapter III
Full tanks and different angles
How much of a reaction was already there before the conversation started, and which angle each of you sees things from. It is the idea behind WP-02, below.

🔧 Tools (2 hours)

WP-02
How much are you carrying? (WP-02)
Each of you fills it in daily for 7 days, about yourself only, on a 0 to 4 scale. It separates the load you were already carrying from what just happened. If one person is consistently at 3–4, the arrangement probably isn't sustainable. Chapter III explains the idea behind it.
REPORT-01
The Full Read (Your Week-by-Week Record)
Record what weeks 1–3 produced so the picture builds instead of resetting. Who did what? How many last-minute rescues happened? What's the running imbalance? (A 30-day spread calculator, CALC-02, is planned but not built yet.)

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 4: "Your Nervous System is Data"
Kane, Christian and guests discuss why your body often notices an unsustainable arrangement before your mind does. (~33 min, in production)
📻 Podcast Episode 5: "The Extraction Cycle in Slow Motion"
How invisible work grows into resentment. Real household examples from listeners. What extraction looks like at each stage. (~28 min, in production)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Shooting Star" — soft, floating instrumental (3:27)
Optional. Something airy to listen to on a heavy day, or while you use the Calm-Down Kit (WP-11). It’s in Brain Breakers.
Expected Output by End of Week 3
You've read Chapters III and IV. You have your first weeks recorded in REPORT-01. You have 7 days of Battery readings. You can see patterns: Who's carrying more? When does stress spike? Where are the extraction cycles hiding? You're ready to talk about communication.
Week 4

Communication & Tone

Talk about the ledger without putting each other on the defensive. Separate tone from content.

📖 Read (1 hour)

Guide
Wired Differently & the Signal Translator
How to open the ledger conversation without starting a fight. Wired Differently shows how the same words land for different kinds of wiring. The Signal Translator lets you test your opening sentence before you say it. If a talk has already gone sideways, the Carrier Wave Decoder helps you find where it slipped.

🔧 Tools (3 hours)

WP-09
Say It So It Lands
Learn to separate what someone means from how it came out. When someone close to you says "You never help," the tone underneath says "I'm exhausted and invisible." The filter separates content from delivery. Practice turning a heated reaction into plain facts. Do this with the other person if you can. It's a tool for talking together.
WP-11
The Calm-Down Kit
Agree on your pause line on a calm day: what each of you says, and does, when a talk gets too hot to continue, and when you'll come back. Decide in advance what settles you, so it's ready when you need it.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 6: "Opening the Ledger: How to Talk About the Numbers"
Kane and Christian interview couples who've successfully opened their shared ledger. What worked? What failed? What did they wish they'd known? (~35 min, in production)
📻 Podcast Episode 7: "Tone Matters: Separating Content from Delivery"
A deep dive into the language of WP-09. Why tone is data. How to hear it without defensiveness. How to notice your own inflection. (~29 min, in production)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Thunderous Shimmer" — curious, textured instrumental (2:51)
Optional. A little gentle alertness before you sit down to talk numbers, if you like to listen. It’s in Brain Breakers.
Expected Output by End of Week 4
You've read Wired Differently and tested a sentence in the Signal Translator. You can run WP-09 (Say it so it lands) on a real conversation. You've agreed on your pause line with WP-11. You're ready to talk to the other person about the ledger in calm, non-defensive language.
Week 5

Frequency Alignment & Synchronization

Notice where your paces differ. Build a small daily habit that keeps you in step.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Guides
The Frequency Framework & Check-ins
Return to the frequency metaphor at greater depth: the five rhythms of money, rest, decisions, talking and values. How do two people with different processing speeds stay in step? Check-ins gives you the steps for one hard topic, once the setting is right.

🔧 Tools (2.5 hours)

Tool
Frequency Calibration
Map your own processing speed, how often you like to talk, and how fast you think things through. Where do you and the other person differ? Does one of you jump into action quickly while the other needs time? Do you have different ideas of what counts as urgent? Understanding comes first. (A fuller Carrier Frequency Audit, WP-12, is designed but not written yet.)
WP-13
The 90-Second Check-In
Once you've seen the mismatch, use the daily check-in to stay in step: ninety seconds, one update each (load, appreciation, friction, ask), with no debate and no rebuttal. Try it together at least once this week, then keep it daily. Bigger topics go to a full check-in, not the daily one.

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 8: "Processing Speed: Why You Mobilize Faster Than They Do"
Kane and Christian explore pace differences, how fast each person gets stirred up, and why one person's "when you get a chance" is another person's immediate emergency. (~32 min, planned)
📻 Podcast Episode 9: "The Phase-Locked Loop: Getting In Sync When You're Out of Sync"
A practical walkthrough of WP-13: ninety seconds a day, no debate, and how that small habit keeps two people in step. Includes real examples. (~26 min, planned)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 The Breathe button — Deep, Ocean, Soft rain or Singing bowls
Optional. Made in your browser as you listen, so they never repeat. Press Breathe at the bottom of any page and pick a sound. Play one quietly in the background if you like.
Expected Output by End of Week 5
You've read the Frequency Framework and Check-ins. You've used Frequency Calibration and identified your mismatches. You've practiced the 90-second daily check-in (WP-13) at least once with the other person. You can feel the difference between being out of step and being back in step. You're ready for long-term stability work.
Week 6

Long-Term Stability

Build habits you can keep. Stay in step over months and years.

📖 Read (1.5 hours)

Guide
Turning Toward
Six weeks in, you've built the foundation. But the framework isn't a sprint to the finish. It's a living system. How do you stay close as circumstances change, as kids grow and careers shift? Turning Toward has small, everyday practices and rituals that keep a relationship steady. (A chapter on long-term rhythms, Chapter VII, is planned.)

🔧 Tools (2 hours)

WP-01, again
Who did what, Run a Second Time
Run a second week of Who did what and compare it with week 1: the same log, run twice. As you read it, remember that not all relational stress is relational. Poor sleep, money pressure and work burnout are outside static, not structural debt between you. Naming them separately stops outside pressure from turning into blame. (A dedicated Static Dampening Log, WP-14, is designed but not written yet.)
Ritual
Your Weekly Calibration Ritual
Design your weekly calibration ritual. What shared moments, for body, mind and heart, will keep you in step? A weekly check-in? A walk? A joint review of the ledger? Make it sustainable, not another chore. If you like, take a first monthly look-back with WP-04. (A long-term Harmonic Resonance workpaper, WP-15, is designed but not written yet.)

📻 Listen (1.5 hours)

📻 Podcast Episode 10: "External Static: When the Stress Isn't Relational"
Kane and Christian interview therapists and couples about separating structural relational issues from external life pressure. How to support someone through burnout without confusing it with abandonment. (~30 min, planned)
📻 Podcast Episode 11: "Building a Ritual: How to Stay in Resonance for the Long Term"
Closing episode. Listeners share their weekly rituals: some silly, some serious, all of them useful. How to make the framework part of your actual life, not a separate project. (~24 min, planned)

🎵 Audio Reset

🎵 "Watching a Shooting Star" — expansive, cinematic instrumental (3:38)
Optional. Something with forward motion to walk with, if you want music as part of your weekly ritual. It’s in Brain Breakers.
Expected Output by End of Week 6
You've read the Preface and Chapters I to IV, plus the guides. You've used the core live workpapers (WP-01, 02, 03, 09, 11 and 13) and run WP-01 a second time to compare with week 1. You have a designed weekly calibration ritual. You understand the framework as a living practice, not a one-time audit. You're ready to maintain this over months and years. Congratulations: the 6-week curriculum is complete.

What happens after week 6? You have two paths:

Path 1: Ongoing application. Keep using WP-01 and WP-02 weekly. Run your calibration ritual. Refresh your setup score monthly, alongside WP-04. The framework becomes part of how you communicate and stay in balance.

Path 2: Deep dives. Read Chapter V, The monthly look-back, and return to any chapter that spoke to you. Chapters VI–XII are still being written. They will explore topics such as households and teams of three or more, advanced frequency analysis and seasonal rhythms. The full program gives you the theory and tools to customize the framework for your specific situation.
