Can’t find your TV? Other live ways to watch

Every way below shows the real, live Sleeping Pals Cam, not a recording. The Cast button sends your timer, sound and snoring choices, your fur babies and your sound mixes to the TV, so it shows your room.

  • Chrome on a computer: open this page, then the ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Cast…, choose Sources → Cast tab, pick your TV, and press Start the Sleeping Pals Cam.
  • Android phone: swipe down twice, tap Screen Cast (or Smart View), pick your TV, then press Start the Sleeping Pals Cam here.
  • iPhone or iPad with an Apple TV or AirPlay TV: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, pick your TV, then press Start the Sleeping Pals Cam.
  • A TV with a web browser, or a computer plugged into the TV: open this page on it and press Start the Sleeping Pals Cam.
  • The Cast button above needs the TV and your phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi. If the TV still doesn’t show up, restart the Chromecast and try again in a few minutes.

The sound starts very softly and stays low. When the timer ends, it fades out slowly and the room goes dark. On a TV it fades out the same way, and the TV stays on a dark screen until you stop casting.
