How it works, in three steps

  1. Check in with yourself.

    How tired, stretched or stressed are you already? A lot of reactions come from what you were carrying before the moment started.

    Dig deeper: what you’re carrying

  2. Write down what’s really happening.

    Each person keeps their own short log of who did what. Seen side by side, the work nobody noticed finally shows up.

    Dig deeper: the work nobody sees

  3. Talk it through from shared facts.

    Hard conversations go better when they start from the same page. The guides and tools here help you pick the moment, the words and the close.

    Dig deeper: where the static between two people comes from
