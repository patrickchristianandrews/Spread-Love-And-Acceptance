The Objective Ledger

Self-discovery first, then healthier relationships of every kind.

The Objective Ledger is a tool for seeing yourself from the outside. It offers an objective, unbiased, outside lens on how three things shape the way you think, feel and react: your life situation, your neurological wiring, and what you discover when you finally look closely.
Once you understand why you respond the way you do, you can tune yourself with intent. That brings a steadier, calmer frequency into your own life, and from there into every relationship you share: partners, family, friends, roommates, co-parents, coworkers, and the people you care for.

Check today’s weatherA one-minute read on your own conditions How it works

People have inner weather, and it changes without asking permission. Seven quick taps give you a forecast, a talk window (open, wait, or closed), what today is good for, what to leave, and a sentence you can send. Log it and your own almanac builds up privately on your device: which fronts keep returning, and how much of it is really sleep.
