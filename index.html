The idea behind it all

See yourself from the outside, then share the load kindly

The Objective Ledger, the method behind everything here, is a way of seeing yourself from the outside. It gives you a fair, neutral view of how three things shape the way you think, feel and react: your life situation, your wiring (how your brain and nervous system work), and what you discover when you finally look closely.
