The Objective Ledger

Understand yourself first. Then share the load kindly.

The Objective Ledger is a tool for seeing yourself from the outside. It gives you a fair, neutral view of how three things shape the way you think, feel and react: your life situation, your wiring (how your brain and nervous system work), and what you discover when you finally look closely.
Once you understand why you respond the way you do, you can adjust on purpose. That brings a steadier, calmer frequency (your everyday emotional tone) into your own life. From there it reaches every relationship you have: partners, family, friends, roommates, co-parents, coworkers, and the people you care for.

Check today’s weatherA one-minute read on your own conditions How it works

People have inner weather, and it changes without asking permission. Seven quick taps give you a forecast, a talk window (open, wait, or closed), what today is good for, what to leave, and a sentence you can send. Save it, and a private almanac builds up on your device. Over time it shows which fronts keep coming back, and how much of it is really about sleep.

Once you can see yourself clearly, the program gives you and anyone you share time, space, work or care with a fair way to see what each of you carries. That way, hard conversations start from shared facts. It is not a scorecard for proving who's right. It is not therapy, and it is not a way to assign blame.
