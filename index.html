Why it’s different

  • 🎁

    Free, with no account

    No account and no paid version. Read every chapter free online. To download a book as a PDF, we’ll ask for your email.

  • 🔒

    It stays on your device

    What you type isn’t sent to us. Erase it any time.

  • ⚖️

    Fair to both sides

    It looks at the setup, not at who’s to blame. Nobody is the bad guy.
