    Who made this

    I’m Christian. I spent my career as a government auditor, and the problems were almost never the ones anybody could see. They were the jobs nobody had written down. Homes work the same way, so I built a place to write them down, and then added games, because nobody should have to be serious all day.

    It’s free, I run it myself, and nothing you type leaves your device. Read my story.
