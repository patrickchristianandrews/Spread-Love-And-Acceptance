Carrier Wave Decoder

For the moments when two reasonable people fall out of sync. Decode what happened, check your state, practice a guided script, and keep notes on what works. It puts the Radio Frequency Paradigm from Chapter I into practice.

            The Carrier Wave Decoder is a reflection aid built on the Radio Frequency Paradigm in Chapter I. Like the rest of TOL‑OS, it's a heuristic, not a clinical tool or a substitute for therapy, and it measures nothing: its readings describe the answers you enter, never either person. It's meant for everyday friction between people who feel safe with each other. If a conflict ever feels unsafe, reach out to someone you trust or a local support line instead. Your notes, journal, and progress are saved only in this browser and are never sent to us.
