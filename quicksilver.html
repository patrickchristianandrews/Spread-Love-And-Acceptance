Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Quicksilver is free and runs on open models. There's no account, no subscription and no usage meter. You only pay if you choose to plug in a paid cloud AI with your own key.

Does anything I type leave my computer?

No, not unless you turn on a web search or connect a cloud service yourself. Local chat, images, video and your knowledge bases all run on your machine. The built‑in Usage dashboard shows the real split.

What do I need to run it?

Any modern PC works for chat; a graphics card makes images, video and bigger models much faster. The installer is small — models download on first launch (about 38 GB for the full Liquid Edition; you can start lighter).

How does Hugging Face fit in?

Quicksilver runs open models and apps from Hugging Face. You can rate models for your exact PC, download GGUF chat models and image/video models in a click, and even run Gradio Spaces without leaving the app.

What's Pocket Pals?

The Android companion app — private AI chat and your studio tools on your phone, pairing with Quicksilver on your PC. It's coming soon to Google Play; this page will link to it as soon as it's live.

Can it do uncensored / unfiltered models?

Yes — Quicksilver supports open, uncensored and abliterated models for adults, run entirely on your own hardware. You're in control of what you install.
