How to read this. Each bar is a plain count divided by the total the framework was designed around — twelve chapters, fifteen workpapers, seven calculators. Nothing is weighted by difficulty, and no component is marked complete before it goes live.

Totals never shrink. A component is never removed from a total to raise a percentage. If the design ever changes, the change is stated openly rather than hidden.

Audio is not metered here. The soundscape catalog has no planned total to divide by, so it has no honest percentage. It grows one finished piece at a time and is listed in the catalog rather than counted here.

The Suite Index is the source of truth. Where this meter and any other page disagree, the Suite Index is correct. It names every component individually. The Content Roadmap covers what is being worked on next. Release order and timing are estimates, not commitments.
