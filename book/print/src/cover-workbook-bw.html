Spread Love & Acceptance

Every workpaper from the Spread Love & Acceptance books is free to print or fill out on screen at spreadloveandacceptance.com. This is the bound edition, for anyone who would rather have them all in one place, on big pages, with a pencil. Inside are blank copies of all ten: a quick check of how much you’re carrying, a calm-down kit you make on a calm day, a self-check before you reply to something that stung, a 90-second daily check-in, a one-week log of who did what, a monthly look-back and more, plus a paper version of the setup score. Each one starts with a short page on when to use it, how long it takes and which book explains it. Then come spare copies, a six-week planner and pages for your own notes. The sheets describe what happened, never who someone is.
