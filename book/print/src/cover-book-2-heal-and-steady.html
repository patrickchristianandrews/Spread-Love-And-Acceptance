Spread Love & Acceptance · Book 2

Some days are heavy. Stress piles up, anger flares, worry keeps you awake, or a loss changes everything. This book is about steadying yourself through all of it, gently and without blame. It explains what stress does to the body and why it adds up, what anger is for and what helps in the hot minutes, how worry, low mood and trauma work, and when and where to get help. It walks with you through grief in its many forms, through repair and apologies that land, and through the slow work of change, one small step at a time. It ends with what matters to you, and what a good life can mean. Each chapter has words you could use and one small thing to try. Where a chapter touches hard things, it says where to find help. Go at your own pace.
