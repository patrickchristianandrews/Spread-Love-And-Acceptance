Is the split working?

Simple versionRead the full chapter

One question: can the way you split the work keep going, or is one of you quietly going under?

Any given week

One of you is quietly doing eleven small things a day that the other has never had to think about. Nobody is lying when they say it feels even. From inside each person’s own week, it does.

  1. Look at the month, not last night.

    When people ask “is this working?”, the answer usually comes from whatever happened most recently. This chapter asks a calmer question, using what you both wrote down.

    Dig deeper: what the check is for

  2. Three things go in.

    How evenly the hours are shared. Whether every regular job has one clear owner. And how stressed each of you already is, from everything else going on.

    Dig deeper: where each number comes from

  3. One number comes out, from 0 to 1.

    Above 0.7, the setup is holding. From 0.4 to 0.7, something is drifting, usually who owns what. Below 0.4, the setup needs to change.

    Dig deeper: the formula and what each range means

  4. It judges the setup, never a person.

    The number isn’t a verdict on who’s better or whether the relationship works. It’s where a conversation starts, never how one ends.

    Dig deeper: what the number is not
