One word. One very crowded house.

Wren Alvarez had one rule about her bedroom, written in glitter pen on the door: KNOCK FIRST. Then Grandpa Lou moves in, her room becomes his, and Wren ends up sharing a room with her little brother Theo, who wears headphones at dinner, counts the ceiling tiles when things get loud, and takes everything exactly the way it is said.

At the same time, the group chat blows up. One word, “fine”, lands like a slap. A group project turns into a shouting match. And the one person Wren needs to say sorry to may need some room first.

Funny, warm and honest, with two dogs named Tidbit and Sugarfoot, a grandfather who has opinions about weather, and a map for the days when your body is loud and your words come out wrong. Short “Try this” boxes at the end of every chapter.
