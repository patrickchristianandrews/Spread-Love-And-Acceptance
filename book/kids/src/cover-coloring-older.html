Take a calm minute.

Thirty-two detailed scenes with borders, patterns and a short phrase in outline letters to color in: breathe slowly, take a minute, say what you need, say sorry and make it right.

Tidbit, Sugarfoot, Professor Puddles, the Frequency Buddies and the clouds of Static are all here. Color a whole page, or just the part you like. Every picture has a blank page behind it, so markers will not soak through.

A calm, screen-free break for a rainy afternoon, and a good companion to the chapter book Turn Down the Static.
