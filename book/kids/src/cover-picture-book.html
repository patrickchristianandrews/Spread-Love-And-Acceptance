Zoom. Slow. Together.

Tidbit zooms. Sugarfoot floats. Today is picnic day, and Tidbit is sure she can carry the whole basket by herself.

Then her paws get buzzy, her ears get hot, and a little gray cloud of Static drifts in. With help from Professor Puddles and a hill full of friends, Tidbit learns to listen to her body, ask for a hand, say sorry when she bumps the basket, and make it right.

A warm, funny read-aloud with a calm-down trick kids can copy, and a page of gentle talking points for grown-ups.
