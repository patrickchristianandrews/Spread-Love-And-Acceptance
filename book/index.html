The Objective Ledger

Understanding yourself and the people you share a life with

By Christian

A ledger is just a notebook where you write down what happened, so you can look at it calmly later. That’s the whole idea of this book: see what is really going on, in yourself and between people, without blaming anyone, and then change one small thing.

Start reading: Part One, Then The whole book on one page Download as PDF
  • Read it in order, like any book. 26 chapters in three parts, and an afterword. Every chapter ends with what to remember, the chapters it connects to, and the next one.
  • Two sizes of every chapter. The short version takes about 10 to 15 minutes. The full version adds the research, more examples and more to try.
  • Free, with nothing to sign up for. No account and no paid version. Read on a phone, a computer or on paper.
  • Or use it with the program. Each chapter ends with the steps and worksheets that go with it, if you want to put it to work.
