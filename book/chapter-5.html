The monthly look-back

Simple versionRead the full chapter

Once a month, look back for the problems that keep coming back, and fix the setup instead of having the same argument again.

The fourth Tuesday in a row

The bins again. You could have the same ten-minute row about Tuesday, or you could notice that Tuesday has now happened four times and ask what it is about Tuesdays.

  1. Weekly check-ins miss patterns.

    One missed job looks like a fluke. The same job missed four weeks running is a pattern, and you only see it when you look at the whole month.

    Dig deeper: why once a month

  2. A problem that keeps coming back usually has no real owner.

    It gets patched every week and never fixed. The fix is to give it a name that sticks, or to talk honestly about whether anyone has the capacity for it.

    Dig deeper: spotting the gaps that keep reopening

  3. It’s not a review of either person.

    You don’t add up old hours or settle scores. The only question is where the setup failed, and what changes next month.

    Dig deeper: what the audit is for, and what it isn’t

Try this: Put a 20-minute date in the calendar, once a month.
