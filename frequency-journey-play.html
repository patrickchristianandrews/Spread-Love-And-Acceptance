How to play
  • Every level is a different challenge. Eighteen of them across the first six worlds, each one fitted to its world: lantern riddles, a walk through the dark, a slow breath, thawing words, ice and water, putting steps in order, a feelings memory match, kinder words, a meadow to bloom, spotting small reaches, sharing the load fairly, bridge plates, echoing a tune, sorting fact, feeling and ask, finishing a line, telling what you saw from the story you added, choosing what would help most, and guiding a friend in the dark.
  • They grow a little harder as you climb. The leaves on each level (one to five) show how challenging it is: short and easy in the first world, more thoughtful at the summit.
  • A new set each time you play. The riddles, words, scenes, sentences and questions are drawn fresh from big pools every time, skipping ones you’ve already seen on this device; the meadow, the tune and the dark path are made new each play, and the walking levels pick one of several boards. Each set is spread across the Five Pillars, with things to notice in yourself and between you and other people. Replay a level you’ve finished and it leans a little more thoughtful.
  • There’s no way to lose. A wrong answer just gets a gentle why and another try. There are no lives or hearts to run out. Hint and Start again (or Restart on walking levels) are always there.
  • Walking levels: bring Sugarfoot (the one with the white feet) and Tidbit (the one with the mask) to the glowing tone together. Swipe, use the arrow pad or arrow keys (or W A S D). Switch (E) chooses who moves and Together (T) moves both. Undo is Z, Restart is R and Hint is H.
  • Everything works by tap or keyboard. Tab to any button; type letters for thawing words, press 1 to 5 to sing the crystals, and plan calls in the dark with the arrow keys and Enter.
  • After each level comes a small lesson card, with a link to read more on this site. At the very top, choose the three lessons that meant the most to you, and they become stars in the Infinite Sky, the seventh world: a place to rest and play.

The tones are used here as calm themes for each world, not as a treatment. The lessons are everyday ideas, not clinical advice. Your progress stays in this browser on this device.
