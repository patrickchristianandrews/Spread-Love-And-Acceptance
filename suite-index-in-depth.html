The Order to Use Them In

Every workpaper works on its own, but they were built to be picked up in sequence. Used all at once, they turn into paperwork, and that is the most common reason a household gives up in week two.

WP-01 → WP-02 → WP-03 → WP-09 → WP-13

Read CALC-01 once the first three have produced numbers. Pick up WP-04 monthly after that. PROG-01 paces the whole sequence across six weeks if you'd rather be walked through it, and REPORT-01 is where each week's figures get recorded so the picture builds up instead of resetting.

If you're new: start with WP-01 and keep one week of the Field Audit before touching anything else. If things feel urgent right now: WP-02, then WP-09. If the same argument keeps coming back: that's a WP-03 problem, not a communication problem.

Workpapers

The numbering has gaps, and always has. The numbers are slots in a planned framework, and the gaps are deliberate, not accidental. Every live workpaper can be filled in on screen, downloaded as a fillable PDF you can type into or print, or done together in the Workpaper Suite, which lines them up for your kind of relationship and makes one report from them.

  • WP-01 The Field Audit & Neutral RefusalsThe 7-day Field Audit plus 21 scripted scenarios for declining or deferring a task. Produces the workload balance figure. Use it first, then for one week each quarter. Page · Bundle
  • WP-02 The Battery & Stress MeterA five-question checklist that each person fills in about themselves, separately. Produces the autonomic saturation figure. Use daily, and before any hard conversation. Page · Bundle
  • WP-03 RACI TreatyAssigns one Responsible and one Accountable to every recurring task. Produces the ownership clarity figure. Use after two weeks of audit data, and amend whenever life changes. Page · Bundle
  • WP-04 Deficit AuditA monthly look-back across four weeks of Field Audits, classifying each repeat deficit as a structural gap, a capacity issue, or a one-off. Monthly, not weekly. Page · Bundle
  • WP-09 The Tone FilterConverts a raw reaction into fact, feeling and ask before you send it, and weighs what you just received before you answer it. Use in the moment, on one thing that actually stung. Page · Bundle
  • WP-11 The Calm-Down KitA kit of sensory ways to calm yourself, for interrupting a sudden stress spike before a hard conversation. Page
  • WP-13 90-second check-in (Phase-Locked Loop)A 90-second daily check-in (load, appreciation, friction, ask) with no debate and no rebuttal. The one habit meant to outlive the rest of the system. Page · Bundle

What Nothing Here Will Ever Use

Tone of voice, recordings, message sentiment, response timestamps, or anything describing one person without their direct participation in entering it. No score is ever computed from one person's inputs alone. None of this is a validated clinical or statistical tool, and if a number is ever used to end a conversation rather than start one, it's being misused.
