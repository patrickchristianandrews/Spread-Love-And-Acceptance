Self-discovery: understanding yourself

An objective, outside look at your own load, wiring and patterns. Start here, with or without anyone else.

  1. DailyToday’s WeatherA one-minute read on your own conditions: the sky, the pressure, sleep, people load and what’s blowing in. You get a forecast, a talk window, what today is good for, and a private almanac of your patterns.free
  2. WP-02The Battery & Stress MeterFive questions that separate the load you’re already carrying from what just happened.free · emailunlocked
  3. ToolFrequency CalibrationYour natural rhythms for money, decisions, check-ins and recovery, measured before comparing them with anyone else’s.free · emailunlocked

  1. GuideWired DifferentlyHow your neurotype shapes the way words arrive, across 14 kinds of wiring, from neurotypical to autistic, ADHD, anxious and highly sensitive.free
  2. ToolWiring CardPut what you learn on one page: how you receive words, what your silence means, and what to avoid. Share it only if you choose.free

Part 2

Relationship tools: for any two people

Shared tools for partners, family, friends, roommates, co-parents, coworkers and caregivers. Each person fills in their own side, then you read them together.

Understand what’s shared

  1. PrefaceUnbilled DebtWhy the unseen work of a shared life builds up like a debt only one person can see.free
  2. Ch. IThe Radio Frequency ParadigmHow pace, tone and urgency knock two reasonable people out of sync, and how to get back in tune.free
  3. Ch. IIThe Epistemic Verdict Engine & P(Solvency)A simple formula for whether the current split of work is sustainable. It judges the arrangement, never a person.free
  4. Ch. IVDeontological Parity & Sensory GatingAgreeing up front how the two of you will judge what’s fair.free · emailunlocked
  5. Ch. VThe Deficit AuditWhy a monthly look back catches the problems weekly check-ins miss.free · emailunlocked

Talk it through

  1. NewWiring CardFill in how you receive words, what silence means, and how a request should be phrased. Print it or copy it.free
  2. NewThe Signal TranslatorPick how each of you is wired, type a phrase, and see how it might land and how to say it more clearly.free
  3. GuideWired DifferentlyHow different neurotypes receive the same words, with examples for every wiring.free
  4. GuideCheck-insOne topic. No overload, no blame, and no criticism of the person. Say you are not against them, then use the order.free
  5. ToolThe Carrier Wave DecoderA guided session for the moment a conversation goes sideways: find what slipped, settle, and try again.free
  6. WP-09Tone Transducer & FilterTurn a raw reaction into fact, feeling and a clear ask before you send it.free · emailunlocked
  7. ToolMood Arbitrage: introductionHow to shift the feel of a tense shared moment, with one worked example.free
  8. ToolMood Arbitrage: full toolkitFive scenarios across different relationships, and a four-week practice plan.free · emailunlocked

Share the work fairly

  1. ToolThe Lemonade StandList the week’s tasks and each person’s hours, and see the split as you type.free
  2. WP-01The Daily Balance Sheet & Neutral RefusalsA week’s log of who actually did what, plus kind scripts for saying no.free · emailunlocked
  3. WP-03Domestic RACI TreatyOne named owner for every recurring task, so it stops being renegotiated.free · emailunlocked
  4. WP-04Unbilled Deficit AuditA monthly review that sorts repeat problems into their real causes.free · emailunlocked
  5. CALC-01The Solvency ReadEnter your workpaper numbers and get one plain read on whether the arrangement holds up.free
  6. Fill-inFill-in workpapersType straight into the worksheets and save them as PDFs on your device.free

Stay in step

  1. WP-13Phase-Locked Loop ProtocolA 90-second daily check-in, with no debate, that keeps small things small.free · emailunlocked
  2. ToolFrequency Sync VisualizerA live picture of how a regular check-in keeps two people in step.free · emailunlocked
  3. PROG-01The 6-Week ProgramA guided path through the workpapers, one at a time, ending with a before-and-after read.free · emailunlocked
