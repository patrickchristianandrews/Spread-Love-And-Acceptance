The book: Spread Love & Acceptance. Its main tool is a ledger: a notebook where you write down what happened, so you can look at it calmly later. That is the idea of the whole book: write down what is really going on, and change the setup instead of blaming a person. It has three parts, read in this order: Part One is about you and is where the book begins; Part Two opens with its own Preface and then Chapters I to V, about the people you share a life, a home or a job with; Part Three explains how people work, chapter by chapter, for anyone.
