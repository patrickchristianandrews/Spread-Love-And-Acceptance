Everything in the program

Simple versionRead the full page

Everything in the program, in three parts: tools for understanding yourself, tools for any two people, and where to start in your kind of relationship.

  1. Not sure where to start? Pick what feels true.

    Tap whatever is closest to how things are right now, and you get a shortcut into the lists. It is the same program, just a quicker way in.

    Dig deeper: find your starting point

  2. Part 1 is about you.

    Tools for understanding yourself: how loaded you are today, how you are wired, and what helps you settle. You can start here with or without anyone else.

    Dig deeper: every self-discovery tool

  3. Part 2 is for any two people.

    Shared tools for partners, family, friends, roommates, co-parents, coworkers and caregivers. Each of you fills in your own side, then you read them together.

    Dig deeper: every relationship tool

  4. Part 3 shows where to start in your kind of relationship.

    Pick the kind of relationship you are in and see which tools to start with and which others fit. Each person also brings their own Part 1 tools.

    Dig deeper: where to start, relationship by relationship

  5. An email opens everything, free.

    Everything is free while the program is being built. Enter your email once and every chapter, workpaper and tool opens in this browser. No account or password.

    Dig deeper: signing up free

  6. Everything else is in the site directory.

    Guides, tools, stories, audio, who built this, what is finished, and the legal pages, grouped by what they are for.

    Dig deeper: the full site directory
