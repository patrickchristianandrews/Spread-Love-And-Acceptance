A simple 50-minute lesson

One way to use How feelings work. Swap in any chapter.

  1. Opener (5 minutes). “Think of a time your body reacted before you knew why: a racing heart, a hot face. Just think, no need to share.”
  2. Read one idea together (10 minutes). Show one idea card, such as “Naming a feeling helps you hold it.” Read it aloud, including the example.
  3. Practise in pairs (15 minutes). One person describes a made-up situation. The other says back what they heard and guesses one feeling word: “So you felt left out?” Swap.
  4. Talk it over (15 minutes). Use two questions from the matching handout. Anyone can pass.
  5. Exit line (5 minutes). Each student writes one feeling word they could use this week, privately.

On a projector or a shared screen

  • The plain version: add ?work=1 to the end of any link, for example spreadloveandacceptance.com/book/understanding-1-feelings.html?work=1. You get the reading without cartoons, pups or bubbles.
  • Quiet mode: open Menu, then Settings, and turn on Quiet mode. It tucks away the helpers and moving extras.
  • Bigger text: Settings has a text size control, or press Ctrl and + (⌘ and + on a Mac).
  • Print: Ctrl+P (⌘P on a Mac) prints any page in a plain layout.

Keeping it safe in a classroom

Talking about feelings and families can stir things up. A few habits help:

  • Passing is always fine. Nobody has to share anything personal. Made-up examples work just as well.
  • Follow your school’s safeguarding policy if a student tells you something worrying, and know who your safeguarding lead is before the lesson.
  • Some ideas assume two people who are safe with each other. If a student is afraid of someone at home or in a relationship, these exercises are not for that. Not safe at home? lists free, private help.
  • Help lines: in the US, call or text 988, or call 1-800-799-7233 (text START to 88788) about abuse; in the UK, Childline 0800 1111 (under 19s) or Samaritans 116 123; anywhere else, findahelpline.com.
  • Shared computers: anything typed into the tools stays in that browser. What stays on this device shows how to clear it.
