    Read the teaser

    Night in the backyard. Tidbit and Sugarfoot are watching the fireflies. A low rumble rolls in, and a warm glow rises behind the far hill. Their ears perk up.

    Tidbit: Did you hear that?

    Sugarfoot: It’s coming from… out there.

    The pals are back. A hot-air balloon above the clouds. A rowboat by a lighthouse in the fog. A snowy mountain top at sunrise.

    New places. A secret door swings open in the library. Lanterns over a night market. A glowing sea. Shooting stars from a rooftop telescope.

    New friends. In the tall grass, someone in shadow holds a little lantern and blinks two big, friendly eyes.

    Tidbit: Who’s that?

    Sugarfoot: A new friend…?

    Bigger adventures. Tidbit sniffs a dandelion: “Ah… ahh… CHOO!” Sugarfoot’s explorer hat flies away. “…Bless you!” Then Tidbit races in, slips across a puddle and lands with a splash. Sugarfoot giggles. Tidbit: “I meant to do that!”

    Same big hearts. On a hill at sunset, the pals sit paw to paw.

    Sugarfoot: Wherever we go next…

    Tidbit: …we go together.

    Frequency Buddies. Season 2. Coming soon.

    After the credits, back in the backyard.

    Sugarfoot: Wait… did anyone see where Tidbit went?

    Tidbit pops up from behind a bush in a space helmet and a cape.

    Tidbit: Ready for Season 2!

    Sugarfoot: Hee hee! Of course you are.
