How the program fits together: the chapters explain the ideas and the worksheets put them to use. It also says plainly what is built and what isn't yet. Looking for where to begin? See which part of the program to use.

  1. Each chapter pairs with a worksheet.

    There are five chapters and a preface. Each chapter shares one idea, and its matching workpaper (a worksheet) is where you put it to use.

    Dig deeper: the chapters

  2. A second layer looks at timing and tone.

    These tools look at how things are said, not who does how much. One is built: the 90-second daily check-in (WP-13), a gentle check-in with no debate. Three more are designed but not written yet.

    Dig deeper: the signal layer

  3. Some worksheets feed one number.

    WP-01, WP-02 and WP-03 feed “Is the setup working for everyone?” (CALC-01), a calculator that gives one read on whether the way you share things can last. Others give no score, on purpose.

    Dig deeper: how they connect

  4. No score ever comes from one person alone.

    Nothing reads tone of voice, recordings or messages. None of it is a validated clinical tool. A number should start a conversation, never end one.

    Dig deeper: what it will never use

  5. Some parts are designed, not built.

    Chapters VI to XII and several workpapers and calculators are still only planned. If this page and the Suite Index ever differ, go with the Suite Index.

    Dig deeper: what isn't built yet
