Layer One

The Manuscript

Five chapters and a preface. Each chapter makes an argument; the workpaper it pairs with is where that argument becomes something a household fills in.

The framework borrows its vocabulary from signal processing and accounting because those fields already have precise words for things households experience vaguely — drift, saturation, an unbilled cost. The metaphors are descriptive tools, not claims about physiology or physics.

Chapter I

The Radio Frequency Paradigm

Live

Separates the carrier from the payload: the thing being said, and the tone carrying it. Most household conflict isn't a disagreement about content — it's two people transmitting at frequencies the other can't receive, and hearing static where a message was intended.

Pairs with: WP-09, the Tone Transducer & Filter · Free to read on the site

Chapter II

The Epistemic Verdict Engine & P(Solvency)

Live

The case for scoring an arrangement rather than a person, and the arithmetic that does it. Defines the three inputs the calculator reads and states plainly what the resulting number can and cannot support.

Pairs with: CALC-01, the Solvency Read · Free to read on the site

Chapter III

Autonomic Saturation & the 7 Ocular Vectors

Live

How much of a reaction was already present before the conversation started, and which vantage point each person is arguing from. Separates "how much am I carrying" from "how upset am I about this specific thing" — two questions that feel identical in the moment and need different answers.

Pairs with: WP-02, the Battery & Stress Meter · WP-11, the Calm-Down Kit

Chapter IV

Deontological Parity & Sensory Gating

Live

Why most chore arguments are fairness-philosophy arguments wearing a dish towel — two people applying different theories of what a fair share means, neither having said which theory they hold. Sensory gating covers what gets filtered out before it reaches attention, and why one person genuinely doesn't see what the other cannot stop seeing.

Pairs with: WP-03, the Domestic RACI Treaty

Chapter V

The Deficit Audit

Live

Why a monthly pass is needed on top of the weekly ones. Some patterns are invisible at the weekly scale and only resolve when four weeks are laid side by side — and a recurring gap usually turns out to be structural rather than a run of bad weeks.

Pairs with: WP-04, the Unbilled Deficit Audit

Layer Two

The Signal Layer

The instruments concerned with transmission and timing rather than with workload. One is built; three are designed and not written.

WP-13

Phase-Locked Loop Protocol

Live

A ninety-second daily check-in — load, friction, ask — with no debate, no solving and no rebuttal. Named after the circuit that keeps two signals in step through continuous small corrections rather than occasional large ones, which is also the argument: a minute a day prevents most of what becomes a Saturday afternoon.

Status: built, in the member bundle, and the one habit intended to outlive the rest of the system

WP-12

Carrier Frequency Audit

Designed

Intended to map pacing mismatch — where one person is mid-stride and quick and the other is at the end of capacity and slow, and the resulting conversation produces noise neither of them meant. A difference in tempo, not in willingness.

Status: no text exists yet

WP-14

Static Dampening Log

Designed

Intended to separate external pressure — money, work, illness, broken sleep — from friction that actually originates between two people, so that environmental strain stops being attributed to the other person by default.

Status: no text exists yet

WP-15

Harmonic Resonance & Entrainment

Designed

Intended as long-cycle calibration: the shared touchpoints that keep two people in step across years rather than weeks, and what to do when they've quietly lapsed.

Status: no text exists yet

What none of this will ever take as an input: tone of voice, recordings, message sentiment, response timestamps, or anything describing one person without their direct participation in entering it. No score is ever computed from one person's inputs alone.

None of it is a validated clinical or statistical instrument. Nothing here was trained on data and nothing predicts an outcome. If a number is ever used to end a conversation rather than start one, it is being misused.
