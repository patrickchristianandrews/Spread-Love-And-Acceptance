One short, straight path through the whole idea. Four small steps, in order. There’s nothing to sign up for, and nothing you type here leaves your device.

  1. Check your own weather

    About 1 minute · Pillar III, read your state first

    Before anything else, see how you’re doing today. One required tap, and up to seven optional ones, give you a forecast and whether now is a good time for a hard talk.

    Check today’s weather

    It opens in this tab, with a bar at the top that brings you straight back to step 2 when you’re done.

  2. Read the Preface, the simple version

    About 4 minutes · Pillar I, see the whole load

    The work nobody sees: the quiet, unseen jobs that keep a shared life running, and why they deserve to be noticed. A few short cards, one idea each.

    or open the Preface page

  3. Try one practice card

    About 3 minutes · Pillar IV, tune how you send and receive

    Say it so it lands: turn a big feeling into a fact, a feeling and a kind ask.

    “You never take the trash out.”

    Fact: The trash has gone out late three weeks running. Feeling: I feel worn out when I notice it every time. Ask: Could the trash be yours on Tuesdays?

    Want more? The full worksheet is Say it so it lands.

  4. Log one thing you did today

    About 1 minute · Pillar I, see the whole load

    Just one. Something you did that kept things running, however small: packed a lunch, paid a bill, remembered a birthday.

      That’s the whole idea in small: see the work, notice your state, and say things so they land. When you’re ready for more, the Lemonade Stand logs a whole week.

    What next? If that felt useful, six gentle weeks walks you through the rest, one small step a week.
