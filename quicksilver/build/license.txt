Welcome to Quicksilver
Forms to fit your work.

Quicksilver is a private AI workstation that runs on your own computer.

YOUR PRIVACY
- Your chats, files, knowledge bases, photos and videos stay on this computer.
- AI models run locally. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to:
  - connect a cloud AI service (for example Claude, OpenAI or Gemini) with your own key,
  - turn on web search or a live research database (such as PubMed), or
  - download a model (from Hugging Face) or an optional engine update (from GitHub).
- Quicksilver has no accounts, no tracking and no advertising.
- API keys you add are encrypted with Windows' own data protection.

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
- The Quicksilver app, for your Windows account only (no administrator rights needed).
- Three open-source AI engines it uses to run models: llama.cpp (chat, MIT licence),
  stable-diffusion.cpp (pictures and video, MIT licence) and whisper.cpp (voice typing,
  MIT licence), plus FFmpeg for video editing (LGPL/GPL).
- Quicksilver Liquid Edition, when the "Quicksilver models" folder is next to this
  installer (otherwise it downloads the first time Quicksilver opens, about 30 GB):
  Qwen3.5 2B, 4B and 9B (Alibaba Cloud, Apache 2.0; the uncensored versions are by
  huihui-ai, converted by mradermacher), Lustify (a community Stable Diffusion XL model,
  CreativeML Open RAIL++-M), Wan 2.2 5B and its Turbo version (Alibaba, Apache 2.0),
  Nomic Embed and the BGE reranker (search, Apache 2.0) and Whisper base (OpenAI, MIT).
  The full licence texts are in MODEL-LICENCES.txt, in the models folder and in the
  folder Quicksilver is installed in (resources\starter).
- Your chats, settings and models are saved in your user folder (%APPDATA%\Quicksilver),
  so updates and reinstalls keep them. Models can be removed at any time under Models
  (the Liquid Edition's own models stay while Quicksilver is installed). When you
  uninstall, Quicksilver asks whether to remove your chats and models too.

AI MODELS
The Liquid Edition's chat, picture and video models are uncensored: they have no built-in
content filter. You are responsible for what you make with them and for following the law
where you live. A parental-lock PIN (Settings > General) puts them behind a code.

Each AI model you download has its own licence, set by its creator (for example Meta,
Google, Alibaba, Microsoft, Mistral AI, OpenAI or Black Forest Labs). Quicksilver shows
where every model comes from; please follow each model's licence terms.

AI can make mistakes. Check important information, especially medical, legal and
financial answers. The fact-check feature helps, but it is not a substitute for a
qualified professional.
