# Testing Quicksilver: a 15-minute guide

Thanks for trying Quicksilver. It's a private AI app for Windows: everything runs on your own PC, it's free, and it works offline. We want to know what's great, what's confusing and what breaks.

## 1. Install (3 minutes)

1. Download the installer zip you were sent and unzip it into one folder.
2. Run **Quicksilver-Setup-x.y.z.exe**. Keep the **Quicksilver models** folder next to it, so the full AI model installs straight away.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the installer isn't signed yet). Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

You need Windows 10 or 11 and at least 8 GB of memory (16 GB is better).

## 2. Try these (10 minutes)

Do them in any order. You don't need to finish them all.

1. **Ask something everyday.** For example: "Plan a cheap 3-day trip to Lisbon."
2. **Ask something hard.** For example: "Should I pay off my car loan early or invest? I have £8,000 at 6%." Notice how long it takes and whether the answer is right.
3. **Drop in a photo or a PDF** and ask about it.
4. **Talk instead of typing:** click the 🎤 mic, speak, and pause.
5. **Use a suggestion:** after an answer, click one of the suggested follow-up questions.
6. **Look around:** Create (pictures and music), AI Teams, and More in the sidebar.

## 3. Tell us (2 minutes)

Click the **💬 speech bubble** at the bottom of the sidebar (**Send feedback**). Pick a type, write a few words, and send. It opens a ready-written note on GitHub; if you'd rather not use GitHub, press **Copy** and send it to us any way you like.

The most useful feedback answers:

- What did you expect, and what happened instead?
- Was anything confusing, or hard to find?
- Was anything too slow? (Roughly how many seconds?)
- Would you use it again? What would make you?

Your chats and files are never included. The "details about this PC" box shows exactly what is added, and you can untick it.
