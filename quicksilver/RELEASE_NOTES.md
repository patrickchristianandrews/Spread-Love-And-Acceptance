## What's new in Quicksilver 2.9

- **Memory.** Say "remember that…" and every assistant knows it from then on. Quicksilver also notices lasting things you mention, like your job, your tools and how you like answers. You can see and edit all of it under Settings → Memory, and private chats don't use it.
- **Projects.** Keep related chats together, with instructions and files that every chat in the project can use. Long files are searched, so each answer reads the parts that matter.
- **Edit as document.** Open any answer beside the chat and refine it: make it shorter, simpler or more formal, fix the grammar, or ask for any change. Select part of the text to change only that part. Save it as Word, PDF or Markdown.
- **Arena.** A blind test: two of your models answer without their names, you pick the better one, and your own leaderboard shows what works best for you.
- **Voice.** Click the mic (or press Ctrl+Space) and talk. Your speech is turned into text on your PC. Press Listen under any answer to hear it read aloud, or turn on hands-free conversation.
- **Your phone.** Scan a QR code and chat with your PC's models from your phone, anywhere at home. The chats show up on the PC too.
- **Automatic updates.** New versions download quietly in the background. You choose when to restart.
