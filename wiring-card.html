Wiring Card

Fill in how you actually receive words. The card on the right updates as you go. Print it, or copy it, and give it to the people you live and work with before a hard conversation, not during one.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you type is saved, stored, or sent. This is not a diagnosis, and it is not a contract. When you are depleted, every line gets stricter.

What this is for. A card is a receipt, not a label. It tells someone how to send a request so it arrives. It does not obligate them to manage you, and it does not obligate you to stay available in every state. Pair it with the Signal Translator when you want to test one sentence, and with Check-ins when the topic itself needs a safe room. The explanations are in Wired Differently.
