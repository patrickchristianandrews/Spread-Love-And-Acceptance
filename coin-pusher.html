Kindness Coin Pusher

Tap the machine to drop a coin. Every five coins that fall brings a short, useful tip.

How it works

Tap the machine where you want a coin to drop, or use Left, Right and Drop a coin. The pusher slides back and forth, and every coin that falls off the front edge fills the kindness meter. Every five coins, a short, useful tip slides up for a few seconds, and bigger pushes bring deeper ones. Every tip you find is kept in “Tips you’ve found” below the machine. Now and then a medallion with one of the site’s friends rides in with your coins: push it over the edge to put them on your shelf. There’s no money here and nothing to buy. Your coins refill by themselves, and everything stays on this device.
