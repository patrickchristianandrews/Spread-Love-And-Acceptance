Kindness Coin Pusher

Tap the machine to drop a coin. Push all 22 friends over the edge for the biggest party on the site.

How it works

Tap the machine where you want a coin to drop, or use Left, Right and Drop a coin. The pusher slides back and forth, and every coin that falls off the front edge fills the kindness meter: every three put a coin back in your cup. Now and then a medallion with one of the site’s 22 friends rides in with your coins. Push it over the edge to put that friend on your shelf. Each new friend brings a celebration that gets bigger as your shelf fills, and finding all 22 brings the big finale and a trophy. Once in a while, a short, useful tip slides up (at most one every few minutes, never in the middle of a celebration). Every tip is kept in “Tips you’ve found” below the machine, and the Tips button turns them off. There’s no money here and nothing to buy. Your coins refill by themselves, and everything stays on this device.
