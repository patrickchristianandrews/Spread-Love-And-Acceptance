Kindness Coin Pusher

Tap or drag to aim, tap to drop, and push the little prizes over the edge to fill your shelf.

How it works

Aim and drop. Tap anywhere on the machine, or drag left and right and let go. Keep Drop pressed for a quick stream of coins. On a keyboard, use the left and right arrow keys to aim and Space to drop.

The shelf. A wooden shelf slides back and forth and nudges the coins toward the front edge. Every coin that tips over into the tray counts. Every 30 coins fill the kindness meter and send a prize in.

Prizes. There are 50 collectibles in 8 sets, from Tidbit, Sugarfoot and Professor Puddles to lucky charms, gems and a cozy corner. The sparkle level (1 to 4) only says how often a prize shows up. Finish a set to earn a new cabinet look or coin style. Nothing is ever for sale.

Bonus lanes. Coins that tip into Sugarfoot’s sleepy corner on the left bring a slow, sleepy cascade. Coins in Tidbit’s bouncy zone on the right bring a burst of zoomies.

The tip jar. After 40 coins, the tip jar opens. Every so often a short, useful tip slips out, with a link to the page it comes from. Turn it off any time.

Calm by design. No money, no timers, no way to lose, and no streaks. Calm effects turns down the particles and the shaking, and it starts on if your device asks for less motion. Everything is saved only on this device.
