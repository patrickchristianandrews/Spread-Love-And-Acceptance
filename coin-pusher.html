Kindness Coin Pusher

Tap the machine where you want a coin to drop. The pusher slides back and forth, and every coin that falls off the front edge fills the kindness meter. Every five coins, a short, useful tip slides up, and bigger pushes bring deeper ones. Now and then a medallion with one of the site’s friends rides in with your coins: push it over the edge to put them on your shelf. There’s no money here and nothing to buy. Your coins refill by themselves, and everything stays on this device.
