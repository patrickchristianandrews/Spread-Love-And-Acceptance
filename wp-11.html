The Calm-Down Kit: a plan for heated moments

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A plan you make while calm, for bringing your body back down in a heated moment, so you can finish the conversation or honestly put it off.

This kit puts Pillar III, Read your state first to work. In you: you learn what settles you, and decide it on a calm day. Between you and others: you pause with a clear line and a time to come back, so a break is never mistaken for walking out.

  1. It is for you, never for them.

    One person uses it about themselves. It is never something to hand to the other person.

    Dig deeper: what the kit is for

  2. Decide now, while you’re calm.

    On an ordinary day, write down what tends to set you off and where you feel it first. Pick two calming steps. Agree on one sentence to say when you step away: how you feel, how long, when you’ll be back.

    Dig deeper: making your plan ahead

  3. In the moment, just follow the plan.

    Check your load score (WP-02), say your line, then do your first calming step, like breathing in for four and out for six, or a slow walk.

    Dig deeper: the calming steps

  4. Let your load score guide when you go back.

    Check your load score again. Under 0.50, go back at the time you said. From 0.50 to 0.59, do another round. Still 0.60 or more after two rounds? That’s okay: pick a new time and say when. When you go back, do one small, simple task first, like putting the dishes away.

    Dig deeper: coming back

  5. It is not a treatment, and not a way out of the talk.

    These are everyday settling steps for the ordinary hard moments of a shared life. And your line always includes a time you’ll return.

    Dig deeper: what the kit is not for

Try this: This week, on a calm evening, choose your two calming steps and write your stepping-away sentence.
