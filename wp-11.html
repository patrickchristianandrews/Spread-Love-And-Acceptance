WP-11

The Calm-Down Kit

Simple versionOpen the full worksheet

A plan you make while calm, for bringing your body back down in a heated moment, so you can finish the conversation or honestly put it off.

  1. It is for you, never for them.

    One person uses it about themselves. It is never something to hand to the other person.

    Dig deeper: what the kit is for

  2. Decide now, while you’re calm.

    On an ordinary day, write down what tends to set you off and where you feel it first. Pick two calming steps. Agree one sentence to say when you step away: how you feel, how long, when you’ll be back.

    Dig deeper: setting your defaults

  3. In the moment, just follow the plan.

    Take your WP-02 score, say your line, then do your first calming step, like breathing in for four and out for six, or a slow walk.

    Dig deeper: the calming steps

  4. Let the number decide when you go back.

    Score WP-02 again. Under 0.50, go back at the time you said. 0.50 to 0.60, run another round. Still 0.60 or more after two rounds, put it off to a named time. Then do one small task before anything else.

    Dig deeper: coming back

  5. It is not a treatment, and not an exit.

    These are everyday settling steps. For panic, trauma or ongoing anxiety, a doctor or therapist is the better call. And your line always includes a time you’ll return.

    Dig deeper: what the kit is not for

Try this: This week, on a calm evening, choose your two calming steps and write your stepping-away sentence.
